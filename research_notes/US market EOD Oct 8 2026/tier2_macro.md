- The rotation is consistent with a "stagflation trade": higher oil prices benefit energy stocks while hurting growth/tech multiples, and elevated yields compress tech valuations.
- The hyperscaler capex narrative has been under pressure since summer; today's OpenAI revenue data acted as a fresh confirmation that the demand story may be overstated.
