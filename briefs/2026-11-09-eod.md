# EOD Market Brief — Monday, November 9, 2026

## Market Recap

Equities staged a powerful rebound on Monday as markets recovered from one of the worst weeks of the year for AI and semiconductor names; the Nasdaq Composite surged ~2.2% and the S&P 500 gained ~1.3–1.9% after the Nasdaq-100 fell 6.3% and chip stocks logged their worst session since March 2020 on November 5. The rally was broad across semis, AI hardware, and mega-cap tech, with the notable exception of Nvidia — which fell 6–8% after its board launched a probe into espionage allegations involving CEO Jensen Huang. Cantor Fitzgerald and HSBC issued buy-the-dip calls on chips Monday morning, saying the SOX index's ~38% drawdown from its June 22 record was overdone.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis crushed mega-cap tech today**: SOXX +7.2% (best day since July 30) vs. GOOGL +1.3%. This is a clear **rotation back into AI hardware** after last week's violent unwind — money that fled semis returned fast, while Nvidia's idiosyncratic probe kept a lid on the pure-play AI subsector. Defensives (COST, WMT) almost certainly underperformed as risk appetite surged. The setup looks like a classic "buy the washout" day, not a new bull leg — volume matters, and Nvidia's absence from the rally is a yellow flag.
