UBS argues semis are 25% off highs and not in bubble territory; CNBC is flashing "possible top" warnings. Two very different reads on the same tape.

## Rotation Tracker

- **SOXX** is up 89% YTD but just had its biggest 2-day consecutive drop in months on AVGO's AI guidance disappointment. Pre-market today shows a bounce (MU +4%) but the trend has turned choppy — no longer a one-way trade.
- **Mega-cap tech** is diverging internally: GOOGL and AMZN are the rotation winners; AAPL and MSFT are relative underperformers. META just broke out on its own catalyst.
- **Defensives (COST, WMT)** are quietly absorbing inflows as investors hedge against the oil spike and yield rise. Neither has cleared the 2% bar today, but the drift is real.

**Read**: Money is not cleanly rotating out of AI names — META's cloud announcement is pulling it back in — but the pure semi trade (hardware → SOXX) is cooling while hyperscaler/infrastructure software is getting a second look. This is a **transition phase**: AI capex thesis alive, but the expression is shifting from chip stocks toward the companies spending the most (META, GOOGL, AMZN) and the power utilities serving them (VST, CCJ).
