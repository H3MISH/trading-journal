# EOD Market Brief — July 7, 2026

## Market Recap
US stocks fell Tuesday as Samsung's Q2 operating profit — a 19-fold year-over-year surge to ~$58 billion — still disappointed investors who had set an impossibly high AI-spending bar, triggering broad profit-taking in global chip shares from Seoul to New York. Compounding the selloff, Reuters broke news that China's DeepSeek is developing its own inference AI chip, deepening fears that AI hardware demand could erode faster than the market has priced. The Nasdaq led declines, falling 1.16% to 25,818.69; the S&P 500 lost 0.45% to 7,503.85; the Dow gave back 0.25% to 52,925.15. Bloomberg characterized the session as a rotation trigger into "less loved sectors," with defensives and consumer names absorbing capital fleeing high-premium AI chip trades.
