# EOD Brief: 2026-07-02

## Market Recap
A dismal June jobs report — 57K payrolls vs. 110–115K expected — triggered a semiconductor-led tech rout on the last session before the July 4th holiday weekend. The S&P 500 finished −0.2% at 7,483 and the Nasdaq fell 0.7% to 26,040, but the real damage was in chips: SOXX collapsed 6.4% as AI infrastructure stocks came under heavy pressure. Fed Chair Warsh eased some macro fear by saying "inflation risks have come down," but couldn't offset the weak growth signal; the rate-cut narrative is fully re-engaged heading into the July 28–29 FOMC meeting. Market is closed Friday July 3 (Independence Day); next session is Monday July 6.

## Rotation Tracker
Money rotated hard **out of semis and AI mega-caps** today. SOXX −6.4% vs. Nasdaq 100 −1.5% vs. S&P 500 −0.2% tells the story cleanly: the AI/chip trade repriced while the broader market barely moved. Financials and utilities provided the ballast; VST held, and bank stocks likely added fractional gains on the lower-rates-for-longer re-read of the jobs miss. Defensives (COST, WMT) had no specific news and likely held. This is **not** a broad market breakdown — it is the AI-infrastructure premium being taken out. The key question for next week: does the rate-cut narrative (weak jobs → cuts → tech re-bid) rescue the semi trade, or does the growth scare dominate and keep the rotation going?
