## Rotation Tracker

SOXX was hit hard Monday (–4.3%, 581→556) on Iran geopolitical news, then staged a
**selective** recovery Tuesday on the CPI beat. AMD +4% and MU +5% led; AVGO –4% after
good earnings and MRVL fading from its intraday high show the "buy fundamentals" trade has
gotten crowded. Mega-cap tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) drifted slightly positive, consistent
with the broad market — no meaningful rotation into or out of AI software names. Defensives
(WMT flat, COST stable) continued their year-long YTD outperformance without a fresh
catalyst.

**Net read**: Money is selectively returning to AI memory and GPU compute (MU, AMD) while
rotating *out of* networking/custom silicon semis on any strength (AVGO, MRVL sell-offs on
good or recovering news). This is **not** a broad semi recovery — it's a bifurcation inside
the sector between compute/memory and everything else.
