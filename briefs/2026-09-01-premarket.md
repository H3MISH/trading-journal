## Rotation Tracker

**Money is rotating OUT of both semis and mega-cap tech simultaneously.** This is not a clean semi→big-tech swap; it's a de-risking event driven by Warsh's hawkish pivot. SOXX (semis) is in bear market territory and leading to the downside; mega-cap tech (MSFT -1.4%, GOOGL/AAPL both lower) is following with a lag. Defensives (COST, WMT) are not prominently bid — this looks more like duration compression across the board than a classic risk-off rotation into safety.
