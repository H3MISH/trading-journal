# EOD Brief — August 13, 2026

## Market Recap
Stocks rallied broadly as the July CPI print came in cooler than expected, pulling the probability of a September Fed rate hike to ~34% from ~55% the prior week. The S&P 500 added 0.65% to a new closing record of 7,798.99 — briefly clearing the 7,800 level intraday for the first time ever — while the Nasdaq gained 0.81% to 26,803, led by META, MU, and NFLX. The semiconductor sector was the clear standout: SOXX surged 2.2% to $559.05 on a combination of post-earnings SMCI continuation and aggressive pre-earnings positioning in MRVL and MU, with semis logging their strongest single session in weeks.

SOXX (+2.2%) led the session decisively, outpacing mega-cap tech (Nasdaq +0.81%; META +1.7%, MSFT/AAPL/GOOGL fractionally positive) and well clear of defensives.
