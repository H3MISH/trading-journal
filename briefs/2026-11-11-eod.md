# EOD Brief — Wednesday, November 11, 2026 (Veterans Day)

## Market Recap

Equities staged a sharp, chip-led rebound on Wednesday after CoreWeave delivered a blowout Q3 earnings report — beating expectations and raising its full-year revenue outlook — which acted as a circuit-breaker on the AI-capex fear trade that had been building since Nvidia's accounting scandal broke last week. The Nasdaq rose more than 1.5% while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index climbed nearly 7% intraday, as money rotated back into AI-infrastructure names that had been hammered since late October. The Dow slid roughly 90 points as rate-hike odds for the December FOMC remained elevated following Tuesday's hotter-than-expected October CPI print — leaving a split tape: semis roaring, macro-sensitive blue chips under pressure.
