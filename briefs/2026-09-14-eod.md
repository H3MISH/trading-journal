# EOD Brief: September 14, 2026

## Market Recap

U.S. equities sold off sharply Monday after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a weekend essay calling for a slowdown in AI frontier development; OpenAI CEO Sam Altman backed it publicly and confirmed a delay to OpenAI's 2026 IPO on safety grounds. Semiconductor stocks bore the brunt with the AI chip complex down 4–7%, while an oil spike simultaneously pushed the 10-year Treasury yield to 5%, compressing growth multiples across the board. The only notable counter-trade: cybersecurity names (ZS, CRWD +14–15%, OKTA double-digits) surged on speculation that an AI pause redirects enterprise IT spend toward security hardening.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) took the full body blow** — AMD, MRVL, SMCI down 5–7%; SOXX estimated -4 to -5% on the session, consistent with constituent moves. **Mega-cap tech (MSFT -0.95%, GOOGL/AMZN marginal) held materially better**, suggesting the institutional rotation is specifically out of AI chip hardware, not tech broadly. **Defensives held** (VST flat, CCJ flat) and cybersecurity surged — money is rotating to the "AI slowdown beneficiaries" thesis (less capex → more security hardening spend). This is not a generalized tech selloff; it is a targeted derisking of the AI infrastructure build-out trade.
