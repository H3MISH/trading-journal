## Market Recap

Stocks extended a three-session losing streak on Monday's momentum, with the Nasdaq sliding ~2.5% on November 16 to 24,509 as Jensen Huang's resignation from Nvidia and tightened U.S. chip-export restrictions to Southeast Asia sparked a historic semiconductor rout — the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 5%+, Taiwan's weighted index dropped 6%, its second-largest point loss on record. Tuesday's (Nov 17) session was far more subdued, with markets drifting modestly lower as investors braced for Nvidia's first post-Jensen earnings report after the close; the Dow was down ~0.4%, S&P 500 ~0.3%, and Nasdaq ~0.2% intraday. The Nvidia report is the single most important near-term catalyst for the entire AI trade: a strong print + guidance could stabilize semis, while a miss or margin warning would reopen the November lows.
