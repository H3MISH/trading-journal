## Market Recap

U.S. equities staged a massive rebound Monday, led entirely by semiconductors and AI hardware, after reports emerged that NVIDIA's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO following last week's abrupt ouster. SOXX surged approximately 12–13% — potentially its best single-session outperformance vs. the S&P 500 on record per Benzinga — while the broader S&P 500 climbed roughly 4–5%. The move reversed a large portion of last week's AI-sector carnage: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index had fallen more than 10% and NVDA had shed 23% — its worst weekly decline since September 2008 — before Monday's catalyst.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (+12–13% SOXX) dramatically outperformed mega-cap tech and defensives. MSFT and AMZN were slightly negative intraday while SOXX printed its possible best day on record vs. SPY. This was a pure AI-hardware rotation: money poured back into the chip complex on the NVDA CEO catalyst, not into broad tech. Mega-cap software underperformance suggests the market is specifically repricing the NVDA/hardware thesis, not re-rating AI generally. Defensive consumer names (COST, WMT) likely held flat to modestly positive; they offered no outperformance today.
