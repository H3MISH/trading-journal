## Market Recap

Stocks finished mixed on the final day of Q3 2026, with the Nasdaq eking out a
+0.2% gain while the S&P 500 shed 0.3% and the Dow fell 0.9% (~400 pts). PCE
inflation for August came in softer than feared — 3.4% YoY vs. 3.7% expected —
reinforcing bets the Fed holds rates in October, but the 10-year Treasury yield
continued its climb to **5.29%**, capping equity upside and weighing on
rate-sensitive names. For the full month of September, the Nasdaq gained +1.7%
while the Dow surrendered 4.9% and the S&P dropped 0.7%.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX −0.23%) **underperformed** mega-cap tech (GOOGL +2.65%, MSFT
+1.43%, AAPL ~+2%) on the session. Money rotated into large-cap tech on the
soft PCE print rather than into semis broadly — MRVL was the lone notable semi
exception, lifted by the Citi upgrade. Defensives and the Dow lagged hard
(−0.9%) as the 10-year yield's rise to 5.29% hit rate-sensitive and
consumer-staples-heavy indices. Pattern: **mild rotation back into mega-cap tech,
hold pattern in semis, defensives under pressure from yields.**
