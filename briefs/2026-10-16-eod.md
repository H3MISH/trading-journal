# EOD Market Brief — 2026-10-16 (Friday)

## Market Recap

A brutal week ended on an equally ugly note for AI and semiconductor names: the S&P 500 fell roughly 1% and the Nasdaq posted its worst week since June (down ~4.5% on the week), as investors continued to de-risk from the AI trade. The proximate trigger was Wednesday's hot September CPI print, which pushed markets to price a ~67% chance of a December Fed rate hike, compounding deepening doubts about whether AI infrastructure spending will generate near-term returns. Money rotated hard out of chip, AI-infrastructure, and hyperscaler names into Treasuries, healthcare, and consumer defensives; SOXX logged its 8th consecutive losing session — its longest streak since September 2015.
