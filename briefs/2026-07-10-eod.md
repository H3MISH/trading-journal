# EOD Market Brief — July 10, 2026

## Market Recap

The S&P 500 gained +0.8% to 7,543.64 while the Nasdaq surged +1.3% to 26,206.89 as the AI trade roared back to life on twin catalysts: Meta's sweeping announcement of its new in-house AI chip (Iris, September production) and Meta Compute cloud unit, plus semiconductor momentum from Micron's reiterated $250B US investment plan. Breadth was notably narrow — nine of eleven S&P sectors closed in the red, with gains concentrated in Information Technology (+1.2%) and Energy (+1.8%) — confirming this was an AI/chip rally, not a broad risk-on session. VIX dropped 6.3% to 15.84; volume of 14.7B shares was well below the 20-session average of 22.9B, suggesting the move was more short-covering and momentum than conviction accumulation.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) ~+1.5% > Mega-cap tech (GOOGL/MSFT/AAPL approx. flat to +0.5%) >> Defensives (COST/WMT flat to negative)**

Today's rotation story: money moved aggressively INTO AI application platforms (META +6.85%) and AI accelerator names (AMD +5.8%) while rotating OUT of memory/networking semis (MU -2.2%, MRVL -4%). This is a "quality-of-AI-exposure" rotation — investors are rewarding companies with direct revenue from AI inference/compute today (Meta, AMD MI300X cycle), not just suppliers of commodity memory or networking ASIC that face longer demand translation cycles. Defensives were left behind entirely; the session's narrow breadth confirms this is not a tide-raising rally but a targeted AI-theme trade.
