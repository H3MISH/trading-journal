**Black Friday — Shortened Session (NYSE/Nasdaq closed 1:00 PM ET)**

## Market Recap

Stocks closed broadly higher in a low-volume Black Friday half-day, led by a sharp semiconductor rebound after one of the worst months for AI-linked names since early 2024. The S&P 500 gained approximately 1.6% and the Nasdaq-100 nearly reclaimed Monday's close, though the index remains roughly 16% below its October 6 peak and had briefly entered bear-market territory on November 26. Tailwinds included bargain hunting in oversold chip names and a large MRVL surge on earnings; headwinds persist — Goldman Sachs estimates ~$70B more in global equity selling still in the pipeline next week, Brent crude stands at $92 on Houthi disruptions to Saudi energy infrastructure, and the FOMC meeting on December 8–9 is priced as likely to deliver a rate hike after October core PCE came in at 3.3%.
