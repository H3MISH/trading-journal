# EOD Brief — 2026-09-01

---

## Market Recap

Stocks opened September in a full-scale risk-off session: a global government bond selloff drove the US 10-year Treasury yield to 4.79% (highest since January 2025) — Japan's 10-year simultaneously hit 3% for the first time in thirty years — while fresh US-Iran hostilities in the Strait of Hormuz sent oil above $91/barrel after two tankers were struck Monday night. The Dow fell 0.7% to 53,185.90, the S&P 500 lost 0.3% to 7,686.14, and the Nasdaq slipped 0.1% to 26,370.89; six of eleven S&P sectors closed red, with Information Technology the worst sector at -1.6%. The dual shock — rising discount rates crushing long-duration growth multiples and an oil spike stoking stagflation fears — hit chip stocks and high-multiple AI names hardest, while defensives and select communication-services names offered the day's few pockets of green.
