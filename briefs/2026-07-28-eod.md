# EOD Market Brief — July 28, 2026

---

## Market Recap

Global semiconductor stocks were gutted Tuesday after SK Hynix plunged ~12% in Seoul on fears that China's CXMT — which debuted on the Shanghai exchange this week — will flood the DRAM/HBM market with cheap supply, sparking a cascade that hit AMD (-8.9%), MU (-6.4%), and MRVL (-7%) and sent SOXX tumbling ~5%, putting the ETF on pace for its worst monthly loss since 2021 (now down ~23% for July). The Dow outperformed, rising ~537 points (+1%) on earnings beats from Coca-Cola (+5%) and Sherwin-Williams (+8%) plus a clear rotation into defensives and Dow cyclicals, while the S&P 500 barely squeaked green (+0.02%) and the Nasdaq fell 0.2%. The session's underlying message: the AI chip trade is narrowing — investors are concentrating in NVIDIA (off just ~1%) while dumping second-tier beneficiaries at any valuation that assumes catch-up, and the hyperscaler capex anxiety seeded by Alphabet's -6% earnings shock last Tuesday is now spilling into everything reporting this week.

## Rotation Tracker

SOXX -5% vs. Dow +1% vs. AAPL +0.7% / MSFT ~flat: the divergence is sharp and the direction is clear — capital is rotating **out of AI-infrastructure semis** and **into defensive yield names and Dow cyclicals** (Coca-Cola, consumer staples, Sherwin-Williams). Within tech itself, the rotation is not a wholesale exit but a **concentration trade**: NVDA shed ~1% while AMD lost 9%, MRVL -7%, MU -6%, confirming the market is reducing exposure to everything that isn't NVIDIA. Defensives (WMT/COST sector) and dividend-paying consumer staples were among the day's outperformers per sector data — a textbook risk-off defensive rotation. The AI names are not being abandoned; they're being pruned to one name.
