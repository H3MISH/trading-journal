## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Jensen Huang officially out at Nvidia (NVDA).** The board announced his resignation effective immediately on Nov 11–12, citing an internal investigation and an ongoing FBI inquiry. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO; a permanent search is underway. This reshapes the entire AI-chip narrative: AMD and AVGO are rallying as perceived beneficiaries; SMCI and MRVL are selling off. NVDA reports Q3 FY2027 earnings **Tuesday Nov 17** — the first print without Huang in 33 years.

2. **Hot October CPI locks in a December Fed hike.** Core CPI came in at +0.3% MoM for the third consecutive month (above the +0.2% consensus). Fed Governor Waller has flagged that level as the threshold for a larger move. Dallas Fed President Logan called for at least two more quarter-point hikes. CME FedWatch now shows a December hike at 100% probability; the 10-year Treasury yield has climbed to ~5.12%, Gold fell ~2.8%. This is a headwind for all long-duration growth names.

3. **Within semis, the rotation is from pure-play NVDA ecosystem names to alternative AI-chip names.** AMD up ~40% in a week, AVGO up ~23%. SMCI and MRVL are the offsetting losers. This is not a rotation out of the AI trade — it is a regime change within it, and it may be fragile.
