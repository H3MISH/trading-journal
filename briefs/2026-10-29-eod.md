# EOD Brief — October 29, 2026

## Market Recap

Tech sentiment cracked on Thursday as Alphabet's Q3 earnings miss — reported Wednesday after the bell — hit markets at the open, dragging the Nasdaq ~1.6% lower while the S&P 500 fell ~0.5–0.6%; Alphabet's negative free cash flow of -$9B (vs. +$2.7B expected) spooked AI capex optimists across the board. Semiconductors bore a second punch as Samsung's quarterly net profit missed forecasts, spreading contagion into DRAM/NAND memory names and pulling SOXX down roughly 2%. Q3 GDP came in at 3.4% annualized (vs. 3.1% expected) and the Fed held rates steady on Oct 28 — but the macro tailwind was overshadowed by the earnings-driven AI-capex re-evaluation.

**MSFT** — Roughly flat to slightly positive. Contrast with GOOGL: Microsoft's capex came in *below* analyst projections, reassuring investors on AI spending discipline. CNBC headline: "Alphabet stock drops 12% while Microsoft rises after earnings." MSFT acted as the safe AI harbor Thursday.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) -2% | GOOGL -12% | MSFT ~flat | AAPL/COST/WMT — no confirmed data**

The rotation read is clear: money is *not* leaving AI broadly — it's leaving the names perceived as AI cash-burning with no near-term ROI. Alphabet's negative FCF and unchanged capex guidance was the trigger; MSFT's capex discipline was rewarded. Semis got sideswiped by two separate events (Samsung miss + GOOGL-driven AI-spending anxiety) but held better than pure-play mega-cap AI. Defensives (COST, WMT) likely outperformed on a relative basis given the tech rotation, though no intraday data was available. Pattern read: **selective rotation out of high-capex AI names, not a broad tech exodus**.
