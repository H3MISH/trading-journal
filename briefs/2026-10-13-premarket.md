## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) vs. Mega-Cap Tech vs. Defensives:**

SOXX has sold off more than 7% from its October high, with the sell-side fund-flow item showing $139.8M in gross sales last Friday with no offsetting purchases. By contrast, META is flat-to-green pre-market, and Chinese internet names (Tencent +4%) are outperforming. This suggests a **narrow rotation out of AI-infrastructure hardware** (memory, servers, chipmakers) rather than a blanket tech selloff. Mega-cap ad/software tech (META, GOOGL) is holding better than capital-equipment semis. Defensives (COST, WMT) have no negative headlines today and will likely attract relative inflows if risk-off deepens.

**Read: Money rotating OUT of AI infrastructure semis; mega-cap software/ad tech holding; defensives a safe haven bid if selloff widens.**
