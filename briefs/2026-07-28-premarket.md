## Sector Temperature: ⚠️ MIXED (Chips Risk-Off / Mega-Cap Risk-On)

**Semis:** Risk-off. SOXX in a confirmed multi-week pullback. AVGO guidance miss is the original catalyst; the narrative has shifted to "are hyperscalers actually deploying all this hardware?" Exceptions: MU and MRVL with strong individual catalysts today.

**Mega-cap tech:** Risk-on but event-driven. Stocks have run hard into earnings. Earnings season is the key inflection point for whether this rotation has legs or is a head-fake before chips recover.

**Macro:** Neutral-to-mildly supportive. Lower yields (oil/Iran) help rate-sensitive growth names. Fed hold expected — no surprise there. The market is in a holding pattern until earnings this week reset expectations.
