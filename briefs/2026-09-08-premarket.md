## Rotation Tracker

**SOXX vs. Mega-Cap Tech vs. Defensives:**

SOXX has surged ~112% in the first half of 2026 on AI broadening (AMD, MU, MRVL, AVGO all participating). As of Sept 8, SOXX is trading in the $506–$527 range — a consolidation zone, not a breakdown, but notably off the pace of H1.

GOOGL ($339) is the laggard among mega-caps, while AAPL ($324) shows relative resilience. This is **not** a classic "rotation into defensives" pattern — it's more a **dispersion within tech**, with AI-infrastructure names (AVGO, MU, MRVL) holding bid while consumer-facing tech (GOOGL, somewhat MSFT) digests valuation risk.

Defensives (COST, WMT) are not yet seeing obvious inflows, suggesting this is still a **sector dispersion** story rather than a full risk-off rotation. That said, if the FOMC hikes Sept 16, the rotation into defensives could accelerate sharply.

**Read: Holding pattern with rate-hike anxiety — watch Sept 16 FOMC as the binary.**
