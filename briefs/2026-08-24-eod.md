# EOD Report — August 24, 2026

---

## Market Recap

Stocks fell Monday as a broad semiconductor rout overwhelmed a Dow that managed a slim gain, with the Nasdaq losing ~0.76% and the S&P 500 slipping 0.28% to 7,652.86 while the Dow edged up 0.26% to 53,417.16. The dual macro overhangs driving the risk-off tone were new U.S. economic sanctions on Iran (details released intraday by Treasury Secretary Bessent) and the collapse of U.S.-Canada trade talks — Washington imposed 50% tariffs on select Canadian goods Saturday, and Ottawa responded with retaliatory levies on U.S. steel, dairy, and electronics. Semiconductors led the decline across the board as investors pre-positioned ahead of Nvidia's Q2 report Wednesday and Marvell's Thursday print, with the SOXX ETF tumbling 2.7%.
