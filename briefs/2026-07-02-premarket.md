Defensives are splitting: COST is holding near all-time highs on strong comps and dividend growth — the classic safe-haven bid. WMT is breaking down on comp-sales deceleration signals that do not apply to COST's warehouse-membership model. Do not trade COST and WMT as a pair today.

**Read:** Money is rotating OUT of high-momentum AI semi names after an extraordinary Q2 run. It is NOT broadly rotating into defensives (WMT is breaking). The beneficiaries of today's semi flush appear to be selective mega-cap names with AI monetization stories (AMZN, META) rather than traditional defensive sectors. Holding pattern-to-mild-rotation in a jobs-print-dependent session.
