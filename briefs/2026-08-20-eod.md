# EOD Report — Thursday, August 20, 2026

## Market Recap

The S&P 500 fell 0.7% and the Nasdaq dropped 0.9% as Walmart's rare U.S. comparable-sales miss (-9.6%) pulled all three major indexes lower, even as the retailer beat on EPS and raised full-year guidance. Bond yields rebounded after Tuesday's Treasury-intervention-driven rally faded, with the 30-year pressing near multi-decade highs following Trump's decision not to extend the Iran ceasefire, which lifted crude oil and fanned inflation fears. The session's other dominant story was the second day of fallout from Marvell's landmark Google custom-silicon deal, keeping semis in flux as MRVL consolidated gains and AVGO attempted a partial recovery.

## Tier 2 (Cleared the Bar)

**WMT — -9.6%** (major mover): Beat on EPS ($0.81 adj.) and revenue (+5.9% to $187.94B); e-commerce +23%, ad revenue +38%. But U.S. comp sales came in at +2.6% vs. +3.8% expected — the first comparable-sales miss in 5+ years. Raised full-year sales guidance to 4–5% growth, but the near-term miss was the headline. Analysts broadly said "buy the dip." Primary drag on all three major indexes.

**XOM — likely up >2%**: Iran tensions pushed crude sharply higher; XOM had already gained 4.4% on the prior oil spike and the same dynamic was in play today. TD Cowen raised PT to $168.

**LLY — PT raise, watch for move**: JPMorgan raised PT $1,300 → $1,400, maintains Overweight; Mounjaro international expansion + Zepbound U.S. demand cited. Potential follow-through depending on today's close vs. expectations.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX) underperformed for a second consecutive session, losing another ~0.5–1% as the AVGO-MRVL realignment and rising yields kept the group in flux — but the damage was contained compared to yesterday's sharp AVGO-led drop. Mega-cap tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) fell roughly in line with the Nasdaq (~-0.9%), offering no relative outperformance. Defensives (COST, WMT) were split: WMT's -9.6% implosion dragged consumer staples broadly, while COST held in better. The session's clearest rotation signal was out of consumer staples (WMT) into energy (XOM, oil names) on the Iran headline. No evidence of a broad AI unwind — MRVL's Google deal is being read as AI-demand-affirming; the damage was concentrated in AVGO specifically.
