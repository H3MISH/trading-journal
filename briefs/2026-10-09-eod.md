# EOD Brief — October 9, 2026

## Market Recap
All three major indices closed higher Friday, with the Dow up ~0.83% to 51,654.95, the S&P 500 up ~0.59% to 7,811.54, and the Nasdaq up ~0.64% to 27,366.17, capping a volatile week with modest weekly gains. The recovery was driven by easing oil prices (Brent pulled back ~1% to ~$103.20 after Thursday's spike) and a tech rebound after OpenAI issued damage-control commentary following its Thursday revenue miss (~$50B annualized vs. ~$70B consensus); an additional tailwind came from reports that Trump stated the U.S. would not strike Iran before midterm elections, relieving Hormuz supply fears. Tone was cautiously risk-on: macro headwinds (elevated Treasury yields, still-elevated crude) were not resolved, just less acute for one day, and the S&P 500 is described as "within striking distance" of an all-time high heading into earnings season.
