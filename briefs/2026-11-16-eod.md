# EOD Market Brief — Monday, November 16, 2026

## Market Recap

Semiconductor names led a broad tech selloff Monday as the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security proposed new licensing requirements for advanced AI chip exports to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore, and Brazil — the market's read being another layer of China-bypass restrictions. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) fell roughly **–3.4%** while the Nasdaq dropped **~–1.3–2.4%** (conflicting intraday snapshots) and the S&P 500 shed about **–1–1.9%**; the range reflects sources captured at different times of day. Adding fuel to the fire: Nvidia leadership uncertainty (conflicting reports on Jensen Huang's status) weighed on the whole AI supply chain ahead of NVDA's earnings tomorrow after the close.
