# EOD Market Brief — 2026-11-20

## Market Recap

Semiconductors suffered their worst single-session rout in years as Hunterbrook Capital's short report alleging AMD's firmware ties to blacklisted Chinese entities spread fear across the entire AI chip complex — SOXX fell 8.8%, extending a now-eight-session losing streak. The Nasdaq plunged ~4% and the S&P 500 shed roughly 2%, with Micron and Broadcom absorbing the heaviest individual losses; AMD itself closed down ~10.8% after already dropping 7.7% on Thursday, a ~18% two-day wipeout. Hot October CPI data earlier in the week had already put a December Fed rate hike back in play, adding a macro rate-pressure layer on top of the China-exposure narrative.
