# EOD Brief: September 23, 2026

## Market Recap

U.S. equities sold off for the second consecutive session as the 10-year Treasury yield surged to 5.135%—its highest level since July 2007—after a hotter-than-expected S&P Global PMI reading reinforced fears that the Fed may hold rates higher for longer. The S&P 500 fell 0.75%, the Nasdaq dropped 1.13%, and the Dow shed 351 points (-0.68%), led lower by utilities, consumer discretionary, and communication services. Semis held in better than the broader Nasdaq, with rate-sensitive software names absorbing the worst of it.
