## Market Recap

Markets staged a decisive recovery from Wednesday's hawkish FOMC-driven selloff, with the Nasdaq 100 surging 2.3%, S&P 500 gaining 1.0%, and the Dow adding ~300 points to test its record high. The primary catalyst was President Trump's announcement that Intel will design and build chips domestically in partnership with Apple, sending Intel soaring 10.5% to $133.82 and lifting the entire semiconductor complex. Micron grabbed its own limelight with an 8% surge as multiple Wall Street desks raised targets ahead of its June 24 earnings print, while AMD and Broadcom both advanced roughly 4%, completing the sector's round-trip from the Fed-driven dump.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX +3–4%) clearly led the session, outpacing mega-cap tech (Nasdaq 100 +2.3%) and leaving defensives in the dust. Within the semi group, AI memory (MU) and AI infrastructure (AMD, AVGO) both participated, so this was a broad semi re-entry rather than a single-name event. Mega-cap tech was along for the ride but not driving — AAPL's +0.6% on its own Intel deal news underscores that the capital was flowing into semi names, not back into the iPhone ecosystem. Defensives (COST, WMT) almost certainly underperformed on a risk-on day. Net read: **money rotated back into AI semiconductor names**, reversing Wednesday's Fed-panic rotation. The open question for next week is whether MU's June 24 print extends this or resets it.
