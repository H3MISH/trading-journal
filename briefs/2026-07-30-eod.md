## Market Recap

Stocks staged a powerful reversal Thursday as Microsoft's blowout Azure cloud beat (+43% growth, best since 2022) and Lam Research's earnings reignited the AI infrastructure trade. The Nasdaq surged 2.8% to 25,122 — ending a six-day losing streak — while the S&P 500 gained 1.7% to 7,437 and the Dow added 614 points (+1.2%) to 52,208. The semiconductor complex ripped: SOXX closed ~$502 (+8%), AMD +13%, MU +17%, MRVL +10%, all on a single session's repricing of the AI chip thesis. The notable drag was META, which tumbled ~9% after Q2 EPS missed estimates by $1/share and management raised full-year capex guidance to $130–145B while free cash flow collapsed 91% YoY; the market is drawing a hard line between AI spenders who monetize (MSFT) and those who don't yet (META).

## Rotation Tracker

**SOXX (Semis):** +8–9% — violent single-session reversal after prior week's multiple compression.
**MSFT:** +16.7% | **GOOGL:** -0.9% | **AAPL:** -1.3% — mega-cap tech was deeply bifurcated by earnings.
**Defensives (COST/WMT):** No meaningful news; likely rode S&P +1.7% modestly.

**Read:** This was not a clean rotation — it was a re-rating split. Money piled back into AI *infrastructure* (MSFT Azure, chip complex) with conviction on tangible revenue, while selling AI *spenders with uncertain payoff* (META). The SOXX rip was a sentiment reset, not a fundamental change. The bifurcation between "AI winners who monetize now" and "AI believers who spend now" is the defining trade of this earnings season. Semis held on to gains, but the test will be AMD on Aug 4.
