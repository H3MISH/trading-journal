# Pre-Market Brief — July 14, 2026

## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **June CPI drops at 8:30am ET** — Consensus calls for headline to fall from 4.2% to 3.9% YoY on the back of June's gasoline price drop. This is the last major inflation print before the July 29 FOMC meeting. A miss to the upside could push the Fed's hand; a beat doesn't undo the oil shock already in motion. The number lands before regular session open and sets the tone for everything else today.

2. **Iran/Hormuz ceasefire has collapsed** — Weekend strikes between the U.S. and Iran unraveled the truce. Trump re-imposed a blockade on Iranian ships and announced a **20% toll on all Strait of Hormuz cargo**. Brent crude is up **~9.6% to $83.30**; WTI up **~9.4% to $78.14**. This is a reflationary shock arriving just as June CPI was supposed to confirm disinflation. Stagflation risk is the emerging theme: June jobs were already weak (+57K, unemployment 4.2%), and oil is now re-accelerating. Fed rate-hike odds for July 29 jumped from 26.7% to 39%. Yields are up (10-year +4bps, 2-year +6bps).

3. **AVGO's $30B Apple deal vs. the chip-sector selloff** — Broadcom is up ~4% pre-market on a renewed Apple chip supply agreement through 2031 (wireless + custom AI silicon). This is the lone standout in a sector that fell 11.4% (SOXX) over July 1–2 and is still in recovery mode. Tests whether stock-specific catalysts can hold against the macro headwinds above.

## Rotation Tracker

**Last week**: Capital rotated sharply from semiconductor hardware (SOXX -11.4%) into mega-cap AI application layer (META +15%). The thesis: AI spending continues but the returns accrue to model operators, not chip vendors — at least at these multiples.

**Today (July 14)**: Chip recovery attempt is underway (MU +3.2%, AMD +2.6%, SOXX pre-market ~563 vs. close ~556), but it's fragile. AVGO is the exception — company-specific deal news rather than a sector re-rate. META and software names are digesting last week's surge. Energy (XOM) is the new outperformer on the Iran/oil shock. Defensives (COST, WMT) are likely bid given stagflation fears.

**Read**: Holding pattern with a tilt toward defensive/energy. The AI hardware rotation doesn't look complete — it's pausing for the CPI print, not reversing. If CPI beats (comes in below 3.9%), chips get a relief rally. If CPI misses upward or oil sustains $80+ Brent, the rotation OUT of rate-sensitive growth accelerates.

## Sector Temperature: **MIXED, tilting Risk-Off**

**Reasoning**: The Iran/Hormuz re-escalation is the dominant overnight driver and it's unambiguously inflationary — Brent +9.6% is not a small move. It clashes directly with the June CPI narrative (which was supposed to confirm disinflation driven by cheap gasoline). The Fed rate-hike probability surge to 39% for July 29 is the bond market's verdict. Stagflation risk (weak jobs + oil shock) is the macro bear case that's gaining traction.

The AI/tech trade isn't collapsing — META's 15% week shows the application-layer thesis is intact — but valuations need either falling rates or blowout earnings to hold. Neither is certain today. Energy names, defensives, and select high-conviction AI names with company-specific catalysts (AVGO) are the places money is flowing. Broad semiconductor exposure (SOXX) remains in choppy recovery mode.

**Risk-off signal to watch**: If June CPI prints above 4.0% YoY, expect broad equity selling, yield spike, and another leg down in high-multiple growth (MU, MRVL, AMD all vulnerable).
