## Sector Temperature: MIXED — Cautious

**Risk-off macro backdrop.** 10-year yields at 5.22% (intraday high 5.36% mid-week) remain historically extreme. Friday's modest yield pullback and dollar softness give marginal relief but do not change the regime.

**Risk-on semi/AI micro story.** SOXX +92% YTD, AI capex from hyperscalers remains elevated, and CCJ/uranium rebound signals nuclear power demand for data centers is real and broadening.

**The fulcrum is October 14 CPI.** A hot print (headline ≥3.8%+) reinforces the "higher for longer" narrative and hits every growth name. A cool print (≤3.5%) gives room for yields to fall and would likely re-accelerate the semi rally. Until then, the AVGO financing headline and residual yield pressure argue for caution on new longs in the highest-multiple names (AMD, MRVL) while the structural AI/nuclear thesis keeps CCJ and VST more defensible.

**Overall verdict: Hold semi and AI-infrastructure positions, respect the yield level, watch AAPL at the open, and stay positioned for the Oct 14 CPI to set direction for the rest of the month.**
