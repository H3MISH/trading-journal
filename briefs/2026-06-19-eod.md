## Market Recap

US markets closed early for Juneteenth (June 19); Thursday June 18 was the final session and ended strongly, with the Nasdaq +1.91%, S&P 500 +1.08%, and the Russell 2000 leading at +2.12%, as chips staged a sharp rebound from Wednesday's Fed-driven selloff.
