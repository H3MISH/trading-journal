# EOD Brief — September 16, 2026

## Market Recap

The Federal Reserve hiked rates 25 bps to a 3.75%–4.00% target range — its first increase in three years — and Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish press conference (dot plot shows median official pencils in at least one more 2026 hike) sent stocks lower from intraday highs. The Dow shed 631 points (−1.21%) to 51,461.90, the S&P 500 slipped 0.45% to 7,551.81, and the Nasdaq barely budged (−0.01%) at 25,978.42, as AI chip names absorbed the news and rallied sharply while software and mega-cap tech lagged.
