# EOD Market Brief — June 22, 2026

## Market Recap

The S&P 500 slipped -0.37% and the Nasdaq-100 fell -0.25% on Monday as the quarterly index
rebalance (five Nasdaq-100 swaps, two S&P 500 swaps) created mechanical selling in removed names
while new entrants Astera Labs (+5.42%) and Teradyne (+4.25%) outperformed. The Russell 2000
bucked the tape, advancing +0.81% to close above 3,000 for the first time in history, as the
10-year yield settled at 4.41% on Iran peace-deal optimism that crushed energy commodities.
Semis diverged sharply from mega-cap platform stocks: SOXX surged an estimated +6.6% to ~$639
(near its 52-week high of $644) while GOOGL dropped 6.7% and META shed 2.7% on independent
negative catalysts, making today a clear AI-infrastructure-in / mega-cap-platform-out rotation day.
