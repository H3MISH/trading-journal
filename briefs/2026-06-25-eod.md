## Market Recap

Wall Street delivered a tale of two tapes on Thursday: Micron's blockbuster Q3 results lifted semiconductors while Apple and Microsoft cratered after both announced hardware price hikes blaming skyrocketing memory costs — a move markets read as margin compression and macro anxiety combined. The S&P 500 finished nearly flat (-0.01% / 7,357) and the Nasdaq fell -0.46% (25,359), unable to hold any lift despite MU's heroic session; the Dow edged +0.14% (51,921) thanks to Caterpillar (+5.8%) and UnitedHealth (+2.7%). May PCE released today came in hot at 4.1% YoY (vs. 3.8% prior; Core 3.4% vs. 3.3%), cementing rate-hike fears that have been building since the June 16–17 FOMC meeting reset expectations.

## Tier 2 — Cleared the Bar (>2% move)

**AAPL (-5.3%)** — Announced sweeping mid-cycle hardware price hikes: MacBook Air $1,099→$1,299, MacBook Pro 14" $1,699→$1,999, iPad Pro 11" $999→$1,199, iPad Air $599→$749, Vision Pro also raised. Company cited soaring memory and storage costs from AI-driven HBM/DRAM demand crowding out consumer electronics supply — the same cost inflation that propelled MU's blowout today. JPMorgan defended the name, calling the market's reaction overdone, but the stock closed near session lows. Gap risk Friday: watch whether institutions buy the dip or continue distributing.

**MSFT (-3.5%)** — Xbox hardware price hikes announced; gaming hardware revenue -33% YoY; overall gaming revenue -7% YoY. Roughly 1,000 layoffs planned across Xbox Game Studios, marketing, and hardware engineering. Stock fell as the gaming division re-emerges as a margin drag at a particularly inopportune macro moment (hot PCE, rate-hike fears).

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis bounced, mega-cap tech rolled, defensives held.** SOXX recovered from Monday's -7.88% single-session crash, estimated up ~+4% today to ~$615, entirely on MU's earnings lift — the memory semiconductor sub-sector is reasserting leadership. But the broader Nasdaq still closed -0.46% despite MU's heroics, because AAPL and MSFT together more than offset the semi lift. Mega-cap platform tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) remains under pressure from the dual weight of AI capex skepticism and now hardware margin compression. Defensives (COST, WMT) likely held in quietly — the 2026 theme of rotation from consumer-tech platform plays toward AI-infrastructure semis and defensives is intact and reinforced today. Money is not rotating back into the Magnificent 7 as a basket; it is splitting specifically into pure-play AI infrastructure (MU, MRVL, SMCI) and non-tech sectors.
