# EOD Brief — 2026-06-16

## Market recap
Stocks rallied broadly after the U.S. and Iran reached a peace agreement expected to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with the Nasdaq jumping 3.1% to 26,683.94, the S&P 500 gaining 1.7% to 7,554.29, and the Dow up 0.9% to 51,671.03. Energy was the lone loser (sector -3.6%) as crude tumbled toward ~$80/bbl on the de-escalation. Under the hood, semiconductor/AI-infrastructure names had a much bigger, more dispersed day than the index headlines suggest — SOXX ripped higher while a few watchlist names (MRVL, SMCI, AMD) sold off on idiosyncratic news.

## Rotation tracker
