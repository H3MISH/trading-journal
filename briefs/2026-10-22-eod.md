# EOD Market Brief — October 22, 2026

## Market Recap

US equities sold off sharply Thursday, led by a broad rout in technology and semiconductors after Lam Research's earnings-call supply-chain warning revived doubts about AI-capex sustainability, a theme that was compounded by Meta's massive 2026 capex guidance following its Q3 beat. The S&P 500 fell ~1.2% to ~7,663 and the Nasdaq shed ~2.3–2.6% to ~26,009, while the Dow held to a modest ~0.5% loss. SOXX dropped approximately 5.5–6.5%, its deepest single-session loss in weeks, dragging the entire AI-infrastructure trade lower.
