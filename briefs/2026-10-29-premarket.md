## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX) are taking the brunt today: pre-market down 3–4% on the index after MRVL -10%, MU -4%, AMD -3.4%. This is not a "sector rotation" — it's a de-risking event ahead of clarity on hyperscaler capex commitments. Mega-cap tech is also lower (GOOGL -10%, MSFT -2%), so this looks like AI/growth across-the-board selling, not rotation into defensives. **Money is coming OUT of AI names broadly, not rotating within sectors.** Defensives (COST, WMT) likely outperform today by virtue of not being in the line of fire.

## Sector Temperature: **RISK-OFF**

**Reasoning:** Fed holding with hawkish dissents + 10-year at 5%+ creates a high-rate headwind. Simultaneously, the two biggest AI capex spenders (Microsoft, Alphabet) posted results that disappointed on infrastructure spending signals. MRVL's potential $83B acquisition and Alphabet's ad miss are the two acute catalysts. With semi names down 3–10% pre-market and no relief catalyst on the horizon today, the setup is defensive.
