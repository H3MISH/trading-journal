## Top 3 Things That Matter Today (Into Monday July 6)

1. **META's AI cloud pivot is the defining story of the long weekend — and it's bullish for the whole AI complex.** Bloomberg reported July 1 that Meta is standing up "Meta Compute," a new cloud business segment to sell excess AI compute capacity to third parties; shares surged +10% and closed at $612.91 (+8.81%). This is a critical narrative shift: it converts $125–145B in 2026 AI capex from a cost center into a revenue-generating business, directly addressing the ROI-audit bear case. It also validates AMD's 6 GW Instinct GPU partnership and signals continued hyperscaler chip demand — watch for AI infrastructure names to re-rate higher Monday.

2. **Macro backdrop just turned materially more constructive heading into the long weekend.** Three things aligned: (a) Strait of Hormuz reopening pushed crude oil below $70/barrel, removing the biggest upside inflation risk of the year; (b) PCE came in softer than expected; (c) nonfarm payrolls printed only 57K — weak enough to solidify rate-cut expectations without triggering recession fears. 10-yr Treasury fell 8bps to 4.37%, 2-yr fell 9bps to 4.09%. Markets are now pricing ~2 Fed cuts by mid-2027. The next binary event is **June CPI on July 14** — with Kalshi traders pricing a 0.2% MoM *decline*, that should be a tailwind.

3. **SMCI faces a genuine headline risk into Monday — Taiwanese authorities have escalated a criminal probe.** Prosecutors raided Super Micro's Taiwan offices and detained multiple employees over alleged illegal Nvidia AI chip exports to China via SMCI servers. Stock is already ~$27.65, down 41% over the past month. This is not a rumor — it is an active criminal investigation with employee detentions. Gap-down risk on Monday is real; it also creates contagion risk for NVDA and the broader AI-server supply chain narrative.
