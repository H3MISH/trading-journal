## Market Recap

Equities posted back-to-back losses Tuesday as the 30-year Treasury yield hit a fresh multi-decade high, keeping broad market sentiment cautious; the S&P 500 fell 0.16% to 7,670.84, the Nasdaq slipped 0.09% to 26,797.54, and the Dow dropped 0.26% to 51,349.92. Semis were the notable exception — SOXX gained ~2.1% on renewed AI infrastructure optimism ahead of Micron's Q4 print — while defensives and mega-cap tech closed mixed to lower. The tape is essentially a bond-yield story: everything rates-sensitive felt pressure while high-demand chip names held up.
