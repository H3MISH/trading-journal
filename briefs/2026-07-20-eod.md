## Market Recap

Markets opened higher on semi-sector optimism but reversed sharply intraday after President Trump announced reinstatement of a naval blockade on Iranian ports near the Strait of Hormuz plus a 20% toll on all cargo transiting the passageway; the S&P 500 finished -1.0% (7,457.69), the Nasdaq -1.4% (25,520.24), and the Dow -0.8% (52,146.42). The standout divergence of the day: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index gained +3% while the rest of the market sold off, with AMD and MRVL leading chip names higher on AI-event anticipation and Alphabet's Gemini server-chip news fueling early momentum. META was the biggest Tier 1 drag, falling -3.1% after Bloomberg reported on Zuckerberg internal comments flagging slower-than-expected AI agent development progress — a direct headwind entering its July 29 earnings.
