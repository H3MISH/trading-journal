## Market Recap

U.S. stocks sold off sharply on Wednesday, the final full session before Thanksgiving, after PCE inflation printed hotter than expected at 3.3% (vs. 3.2% est.) and the Trump administration announced new tariffs targeting 14 major trading partners, spooking growth and AI-levered names. The Nasdaq Composite fell approximately 3.5–4% and the S&P 500 dropped roughly 2.7–3%; semiconductors led the decline, extending Tuesday's losses and pushing the Nasdaq 100 roughly 20% below its October peak — crossing the technical bear-market threshold. Volume was elevated into the holiday close, with no meaningful afternoon recovery.
