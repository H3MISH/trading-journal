## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **MRVL earnings beat is the catalyst.** Marvell reported fiscal Q3 2027 results after the close on Wednesday — beat on revenue and EPS, raised Q4 guidance, cited accelerating AI custom-ASIC demand. Stock +7–8% pre-market. This single report is validating the AI hardware investment thesis and pulling the entire semiconductor group higher after a brutal two-day selloff.

2. **AVGO reports full fiscal year 2026 on December 9 — earnings gap risk is live.** Broadcom guided Q4 revenue to ~$34.8B and AI semiconductor revenue to $21.7B (+236% YoY). With the stock up 4–5% today on relief, any miss or guidance cut nine trading days from now will hurt badly. Flag it now.

3. **Hawkish macro hasn't gone away.** Core PCE printed 3.3% (est. 3.2%), GDP revised to +4.3% annualized, 10-year yield ~4.85%, and markets now price a 96% probability of a December rate hike. Today's recovery is a sentiment bounce, not a macro inflection — rate pressure on high-multiple tech persists.

## Rotation Tracker

**This week's story:** SOXX fell 7–10% over Nov 24–25 as hedge funds unwound a popular semi-vs-mega-cap relative value trade. AI hardware became the market's pain trade — not a fundamental break, but a crowded position getting flushed.

**Today's read:** Reversal underway. SOXX pre-market +5–6%, clearly outpacing mega-cap tech (GOOGL/MSFT/AAPL +2–4%), with defensives (COST, WMT) nowhere to be found in the headlines. Money is **rotating back into semis**. MRVL's earnings beat is the trigger — it answered the key question (is AI hardware demand still real?) with a yes. Capital is flowing from mega-cap software back toward the semiconductor group.

This is a re-entry, not a new bull leg — the same hedge-fund positioning that got flushed will re-load, but carefully.
