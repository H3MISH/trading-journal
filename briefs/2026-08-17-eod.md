# EOD Report: 2026-08-17 (Monday)

## Market Recap

US equities slid modestly Monday — S&P 500 −0.5%, Dow −0.5%, Nasdaq −0.3% — as Brent crude surged to ~$89/bbl and 30-year Treasury yields hit their highest level since 2007, both driven by renewed US-Iran war fears. The macro drag hit mega-cap software and consumer names, but AI infrastructure and semiconductor stocks bucked the sell-off: Anthropic's strong revenue-growth data reinforced the AI capex cycle narrative, sending memory and custom-silicon names sharply higher, with MU +6%, MRVL +7.6%, and AMD +6.5% leading the charge.
