## Tier 2: News Clearing the Bar

**AMZN / AAPL** — The midterm data center narrative explicitly bifurcated the AI trade: hyperscalers ("AI incumbents") rose while chipmakers ("AI borrowers") fell. Amazon and Apple were cited as direct beneficiaries of data center regulation fears — the logic being that existing cloud infrastructure becomes more valuable if new builds are constrained. This is a meaningful regime shift worth tracking; if it persists, it argues for holding the hyperscalers while the semi recovery is still uncertain.

## Rotation Tracker

**Rotation OUT of AI chipmakers → into AI hyperscalers; defensives holding steady.**

The midterm selloff drew a sharp line inside the AI complex. SOXX –23% over the past month vs. mega-cap cloud (AMZN, AAPL, GOOGL) holding up or rising on the same news. The logic: data center permitting restrictions hurt the capex-heavy chip/infrastructure buildout side far more than the incumbents who already own the capacity. Friday's semi rebound was violent but partially mechanical (oversold bounce after the worst sector day in 6 years). Pre-market this Monday shows AMD +3% while MU and MRVL are slightly red — not a clean "back in" signal for the sector yet. Defensives (COST, WMT) have not shown unusual outperformance; this is tech-internal rotation, not a full risk-off flight to safety. The CPI print Tuesday is the most likely catalyst to resolve the direction: cool CPI → AI trade reignites; hot CPI → rate pressure accelerates rotation further away from growth.

**Overall verdict:** Hold the Friday rebound gains with tight leash. AMD's upgrade is an incrementally positive read on sector stabilization, but the structural regulatory story and the looming CPI print argue against adding aggressively into the bounce. The clearest near-term trade setup is waiting for Tuesday's CPI: a cool print opens the AI re-entry; a hot print likely retests Thursday's lows.
