The stock was already down 5.6% Wednesday in pre-market. AVGO reports Dec 9. Both are within the 10-day earnings window. Manage size accordingly.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis vs. mega-cap tech vs. defensives: hard rotation OUT of semis and AI names.**

SOXX is in correction from recent highs. The tech overweight among portfolio managers collapsed from 37% to 12% — the sharpest such move since June 2006 per Goldman Sachs. Clients are rotating into consumer staples (COST, WMT outperforming), utilities, and REITs. Apple is an outlier within tech, holding up better because the market views it as carrying less AI-capex risk than peers.

Alphabet (GOOGL) edged up ~1.1% Wednesday, suggesting some distinction between pure AI-infrastructure plays (which are being sold) and advertising/search tech (which is being held). MSFT had no notable move reported.

Money is not "rotating back in" to AI names — it is actively rotating out, with defensives absorbing the inflows.
