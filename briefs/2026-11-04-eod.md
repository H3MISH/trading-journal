## Market Recap

Democrats swept Congress in Tuesday's midterms, triggering a broad relief rally Wednesday as political-uncertainty risk came off — the Dow rose roughly 2.9% midday and mega-cap consumer tech (AMZN +7.7%, AAPL +5.6%) led the charge. The move was not uniform: AI-infrastructure names (power, data centers, semis) sold off hard as investors rotated toward names perceived to benefit from a Democratic Congress, while AMD and SMCI bucked the semi weakness after blowout earnings. Oil fell more than 8% intraday, pulling energy/power names like VST and CCJ further into the red.
