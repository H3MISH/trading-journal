## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) decisively outperformed mega-cap tech today.** The semiconductor index gained an estimated 2–3% on constituent strength (SMCI +13%, MU +4.7%, MRVL +4%, AMD +3%, AVGO +1%) while GOOGL, MSFT, and AAPL were essentially flat to down ~1%. The pattern: money is rotating **within** the AI theme — away from software/platform layer (META, MSFT) toward silicon and AI infrastructure (SMCI, MU, MRVL, AMD). This is not a risk-off move; it is a sector-rotation trade triggered by SMCI confirming that AI hardware demand is accelerating, not plateauing. Defensives (COST, WMT) were not notable.
