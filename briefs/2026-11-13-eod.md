## Market Recap

Semiconductor stocks led a broad Friday sell-off as markets digested the second full session following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation on November 12 — the dominant macro event driving all AI-adjacent names. The S&P 500 fell approximately **0.7%** (to ~7,536–7,540), the Nasdaq Composite declined roughly **1.0–1.5%** (to ~24,990–25,100), and the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) dropped **4.1%**; SOXX ended the week down sharply from its prior-week close. Compounding the chip weakness was the 10-year Treasury yield reaching **5%** mid-session — a level that historically pressures growth and AI names — as bond markets repriced Fed-cut expectations amid persistent inflation signals.
