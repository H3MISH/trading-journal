## Rotation Tracker

**Current read: Semis fragmenting internally; mega-cap tech is the relative safe haven.**

July saw a violent rotation out of AI infrastructure into value/defensives, followed by a sharp 18% recovery in SOXX from the lows. But today's pre-market is fracturing within semis — memory names (MU, MRVL) are ripping on AI demand proof-points while AMD is getting hit on macro/debt concerns. Mega-caps (MSFT, AMZN, AAPL) are holding flat-to-slightly-green with Nasdaq futures down. This is not broad rotation out of AI; it's **selective profit-taking in high-beta, high-multiple semi names (AMD) while AI-revenue-proven names attract buying (MU, MRVL, AVGO)**. Defensives (COST, WMT) not showing unusual pre-market movement — not a flight-to-safety signal, more of a rebalancing.
