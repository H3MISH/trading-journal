Money is quietly rotating *within* tech: out of semiconductor and AI-infra names (exposed to Democratic regulatory risk on data-center capex) and into consumer/software mega-cap names (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL) that look better positioned under potential policy scrutiny. The pattern is best described as a **sector-specific rotation, not a macro de-risking event**.
