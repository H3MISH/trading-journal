# EOD Market Brief — Monday, June 15, 2026

## Market Recap

President Trump announced a completed U.S.-Iran peace deal today, including an MOU to reopen the Strait of Hormuz (formal signing set for June 19), triggering the broadest risk-on rally in months: oil fell ~5%, Dow hit a new all-time closing high, and the Nasdaq surged more than 3%. Semiconductor and AI names were the clear leaders as lower energy costs ease data-center economics and reduced geopolitical uncertainty unlocked risk appetite. The S&P 500 posted its best single-session gain since April.

- **S&P 500:** +1.65% → 7,554.29
- **Nasdaq:** +3.07% → 26,683.94
- **Dow:** +0.92% → 51,671.03 (record close)
