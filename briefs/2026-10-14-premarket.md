The market is simultaneously selling down chips on fundamental concerns AND approaching a major earnings gauntlet for the AI platform layer. Position sizing deserves a second look on anything directional into next week.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) down hard; META/AI applications diverging.** The chip sector has now shed ~18% from its June peak over several weeks, and the selling appears structural rather than technical — it's driven by China DRAM competition (CXMT), hyperscaler in-house silicon (Microsoft Maia), and AI capex ROI skepticism. Meanwhile, AI application plays like META are outperforming: up ~2.4% on Muse AI momentum while the broader SOXX bleeds. Mega-cap tech (MSFT, GOOGL) is mixed — AI infrastructure investment is being questioned on ROI but actual AI revenue is showing up in platform names. The rotation theme is: **out of AI chips and infrastructure, into AI applications and platforms**. Defensives (COST, WMT) have not been specifically flagged but elevated oil and yields make them relatively attractive vs. growth; no major defensive breakout confirmed.
