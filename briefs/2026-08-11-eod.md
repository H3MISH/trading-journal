# EOD Market Brief — 2026-08-11

## Market Recap
US equities pulled back Tuesday as hopes for a Strait of Hormuz reopening faded, keeping crude elevated (WTI briefly above $84, Brent near $90 intraday) and reigniting US-Iran war fears before Pakistan's signals of a potential arrangement trimmed oil's gains into the close. The Dow lost ~0.3%, the S&P 500 fell ~0.3%, and the Nasdaq dropped ~0.6%, with Communication Services the worst-performing S&P sector (-1% to -2%, led lower by Alphabet and AppLovin). July payrolls came in at -23K — softer than expected and reducing odds of another Fed rate hike — but that macro cushion wasn't enough to offset geopolitical risk. Volume was mixed to light across most large-cap tech names.
