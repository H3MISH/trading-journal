## Market Recap

The November 25 session was a tale of two halves: an AI-led chip rout sent the Nasdaq Composite down as much as 2–3% intraday before a late recovery trimmed losses to –0.6%, with the S&P 500 finishing –0.25%. Core PCE (October) printed at 3.3% YoY — hotter than hoped — reinforcing fears that the Fed will hold rates higher for longer, while fresh tariff signals from the incoming administration added an additional headwind for semis and AI names. SOXX touched its 52-week low of ~$127 intraday before rebounding to close only –0.5%, a show of resilience that masked a deeply negative tape for most of the session.
