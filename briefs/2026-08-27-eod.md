# EOD Report: 2026-08-27

## Market Recap

US equities ended mostly higher Thursday as Nvidia's blowout Q2 print — Q3 data-center revenue guidance of $108B, actual Q3 data-center revenue of $89B — spread positive sentiment across AI-exposed names, lifting the Nasdaq +0.7% and S&P 500 +0.4%. The semiconductor complex was bifurcated: AVGO rallied 4.1% on the Nvidia halo while AMD and MU erased most of their early gap-ups on profit-taking, suggesting the market is concentrating fresh capital in names with explicit hyperscaler contract visibility rather than the broader chip tape. The 10-year yield held at 4.66% with the Jackson Hole Symposium in the background, keeping rate-cut timing in focus through Friday's PCE print.
