## Rotation Tracker

Mega-cap tech is splintering rather than moving as a bloc: GOOGL -4%, META +1%, MSFT flat on September 23. This is not a broad rotation out of tech — it's a barbell trade where AI-glasses/agent-commerce names (META) win while ad-dependent cloud names (GOOGL) give back. Semis are running on their own cycle, decoupled from mega-cap behavior and supported by AI capex demand. Money is not rotating out of AI; it's sorting *within* AI — hardware and wearable-adjacent names gaining relative to pure cloud/search. Holding pattern with selectivity bias.
