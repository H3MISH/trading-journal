# EOD Market Brief — Wednesday, October 28, 2026

## Market Recap

Equities ground out a narrow win as chip strength offset caution ahead of the Fed and big-tech earnings: the S&P 500 added 0.28% to close at 7,743.98, the Nasdaq edged up 0.27% to 26,549.85, and the Dow gained 0.19% to 52,475.10.
