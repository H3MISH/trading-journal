## Rotation Tracker

July 30 produced a **sharp AI trade split** rather than a classic sector rotation. Cloud infrastructure (MSFT +16%) was rewarded while AI application layer (META -9%) was punished. Semis (SOXX +8%) bounced hard from deeply oversold levels. This is **not** money rotating out of tech into defensives — defensives (COST, WMT) were not notable movers. The real rotation is an intra-tech quality sort: **AI ROI haves vs. have-nots.** Markets are paying up for companies where AI investment is driving verifiable revenue (MSFT Azure), and dumping companies where AI capex is rising but returns are speculative (META). Semis sit in the middle — their rebound suggests the market still believes in the infrastructure buildout, even as application-layer economics are questioned.
