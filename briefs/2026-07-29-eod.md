# EOD Brief — July 29, 2026

## Market Recap

The Fed delivered a hawkish hold Wednesday, keeping rates at 3.5–3.75% while three members dissented and voted for a hike, jolting the bond market: the 10-year yield jumped 7 bps to 4.67% and the 30-year hit 5.2%, its highest since 2007. Equity indices absorbed the damage quietly on the surface — S&P 500 −0.13%, Nasdaq −0.11%, Dow −0.78% — but semis were the real casualty, with SOXX down another 3.24% and now roughly 25% off its June 22 peak. Oil surged 6.4–6.6% on renewed Middle East tensions, while investors braced for blockbuster reports from META and Microsoft after the bell.

## After-Hours Movers

**META — Q2 2026 beat-on-revenue, miss-on-earnings:**
Revenue came in at $60.8B (+28% YoY), topping the $60.29B consensus. However, EPS was $6.18 diluted — well below the ~$7.19–7.23 expected — as operating margin collapsed from 43% (Q2 2025) to 31% on spiking expenses: legal charges plus severance from the May 2026 headcount reduction. Net income fell 14% YoY. Q3 guidance $61–64B; FY2026 capex $130–145B. Shares were up ~3% AH ahead of the call, then fell ~4% on the EPS miss and margin shock — implying a gap-down open Thursday if the reaction holds.

**MSFT — Q4 FY2026 (results after close):**
Analysts expected $4.24 EPS and $87.6B revenue (+14.6% YoY). The market's focus: Azure growth rate (guided 39–40%) and FY2027 capex of $255–260B (+35% YoY). Full after-hours reaction was not confirmed at report time; watch the overnight print for MSFT and Nasdaq futures direction.
