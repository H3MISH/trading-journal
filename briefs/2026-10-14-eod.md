# EOD Market Brief — Wednesday, October 14, 2026

## Market Recap

Stocks sold off broadly after a hotter-than-expected September CPI print (headline 3.7% YoY vs. 3.6% expected; core 2.6% YoY vs. 2.5% expected) reignited fears of another Fed rate hike at the October 27–28 FOMC meeting. The Nasdaq Composite bore the brunt, falling 1.88% to 26,692.87, while the S&P 500 shed 1.11% to 7,718.94 and the Dow Jones slipped a modest 0.09% to 51,621.48. Semiconductors were the epicenter of pain—SOXX extended its losing streak to seven consecutive sessions—while AI-adjacent names outside the Magnificent Seven, including CoreWeave (−15%+) and Oracle (−9%), were hit hardest as investors questioned high-debt AI infrastructure buildouts.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis crushed, defensives held.** SOXX posted its 7th consecutive down session (−2.3 to −4.4%), while the Nasdaq fell 1.88% vs. the Dow's near-flat −0.09%—classic flight from growth/AI into defensives and rate-insensitive value. Mega-cap tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) fell in line with or slightly below the Nasdaq, suggesting no clean rotation *into* mega-cap—it was a broad de-risk, not a sector swap. Defensives (COST, WMT) likely outperformed on a relative basis but no dramatic inflows noted. Read: **money is rotating *out* of AI/chip names into cash and shorter-duration assets**, not into a specific equity sector. This is a macro-driven rate re-pricing, not a micro rotation.
