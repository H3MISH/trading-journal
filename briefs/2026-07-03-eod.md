## Market Recap

The final session before the long holiday weekend dealt a sharp blow to semiconductor stocks: the PHLX Semiconductor Index shed 6.7% on Thursday, July 2 as investors took profits across the chip complex after the sector roughly doubled in Q2 — MU, MRVL, INTC, AMAT, and Lam each fell 7–10%. The catalyst was a badly missed June payrolls print (57K actual vs. 100K+ expected, unemployment 4.2%), which triggered a defensive rotation: the Dow Jones climbed 539 points (+1.03%) to a fresh all-time high of 52,844 — led by AAPL (+4.8%), McDonald's, and Disney — while the Nasdaq-100 lost 0.8% and utilities, healthcare, and consumer staples each rose 2%+. The S&P 500 finished roughly flat as the Dow's defensive strength offset tech's slide, leaving the tape bifurcated entering the holiday weekend.
