## Tier 2: Cleared the Bar

**MSFT — ~-1.7%** *(technically under 2% but meaningful news)*

The move is a reminder that the "AI ROI audit" overhang is live at the largest tech names — investors skeptical about near-term margin expansion from $100B+ capex cycles. MSFT underperformed on a day when the rest of tech surged; that divergence is worth tracking.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis back in the lead, mega-cap tech split.** SOXX +2.69% outpaced the Nasdaq (+~1.2%) on a clean holiday-return bounce, and AMD's +8.5% confirms the AI chip names are reclaiming ground after weeks of sector distribution. Defensives (COST, WMT) had no notable moves today; no evidence of risk-off rotation.
