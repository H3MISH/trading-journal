## Market Recap

Stocks posted a broad advance Tuesday as the AI trade roared back to life, led by semiconductor names: AMD surged 5.5%+ on expanded Meta Instinct GPU supply pact momentum, and SMCI jumped 7%+ ahead of its November 3 earnings. The S&P 500 closed near 7,722 (+0.6%), the Nasdaq Composite gained 1.18% to ~26,478, and the Dow was flat at ~52,378; tech and semis were the clear winners while energy and other cyclicals lagged. The Federal Reserve began its two-day FOMC policy meeting today, keeping the market cautious about rate path signals headed into tomorrow's decision at 2:00 PM ET.
