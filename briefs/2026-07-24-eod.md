## Market Recap

US equities staged a split session: the Dow rose +235 pts (+0.46%) and S&P 500 closed essentially flat (+0.05%), but the Nasdaq slipped -0.6% as semiconductor stocks collapsed ~3.6–4.4%. The primary trigger was an overnight Korea KOSPI selloff — SK Hynix fell ~6% ahead of its July 28–29 Q2 2026 earnings — that quickly spread to US memory names (MU, MRVL) and then into the broader chip complex. The selloff landed on top of a sector already in confirmed bear-market territory (SOXX -20%+ from its June 22 all-time high), still digesting Alphabet's $195–205B capex bombshell from Tuesday. Oil eased on Iran/Pakistan peace-talk news, providing partial relief to non-chip equities. New Trump tariffs took effect today. Weekly scorecard: Dow -0.5% / S&P -0.7% / Nasdaq -2%+.
