## Market Recap

US equities staged a partial, uneven recovery from Thursday's brutal AI-capex-driven sell-off: the Dow rose +235 pts (+0.46%), the S&P 500 closed essentially flat (+0.05%), and the Nasdaq slipped -0.6%, still weighed down by a 4.4% collapse in semiconductor stocks. Weekly scorecard: Dow -0.5% / S&P -0.7% / Nasdaq -2%+.
