## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **10-Year Treasury near 24-year highs (~5.33%) after Waller signals more hikes.** Fed Governor Waller said more rate increases are needed, pushing the 10-year yield up 5+ bps to 5.328%, its highest since 2002. A $22B 30-year bond auction today is the next market-moving event. Rate-sensitive tech and high-multiple growth names are under pressure across the board.

2. **US-Iran conflict has driven oil to two-year highs near $85/bbl.** Brent surged ~7.8% and WTI ~10% last week after US-Iranian attacks resumed and disrupted oil flows. OPEC+ kept output unchanged for October. Energy prices add to inflation concerns that are keeping the Fed hawkish. Tanker/shipping risk is rising. Bullish for energy names (XOM) and nuclear alternatives (CCJ).

3. **MRVL investor day afterglow + TD Cowen upgrade to Buy ($350 PT) — but profit-taking underway.** Marvell's stock surged ~6% Tuesday after it set a $70–90B fiscal 2031 revenue target at investor day. Wednesday saw ~2% pullback as traders took profits. SOXX enters October up ~89% YTD and hits its historically strongest seasonal window (Oct–Feb avg. +2.4%/month). Semis are the best-performing sector in the US market this year.
