## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX +2.1%) clearly outperformed mega-cap tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL roughly flat to -1%) and defensives (COST, WMT no notable moves). This is a rotation *into* AI-infrastructure names, not out of them. SpaceX's AI-revenue print acted as a re-confidence vote in the capex cycle that spooked investors in the prior week's sell-off. The SMCI gap-up reinforces that narrative — data center builds are not slowing. META's AH print being taken positively despite the EPS miss suggests big-tech earnings sentiment is still constructive heading into AMD (Nov 3).
