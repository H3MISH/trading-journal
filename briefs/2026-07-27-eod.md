## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) -2.5% vs. Mega-cap software (GOOGL +2.3%, MSFT +2.0%, AAPL +1.2%) vs. Defensives (WMT ~flat, COST data thin).**

Classic pre-earnings rotation: money left the hardware/AI-infrastructure layer (SOXX, AMD, MU, MRVL, SMCI) and rotated into software/platform names expected to *monetize* AI spending (GOOGL, MSFT) ahead of their Wednesday reports. This is not a risk-off move — it's a sector swap within tech. Defensives were uninvolved. If MSFT/META Wednesday results disappoint, expect the rotation to unwind violently back into cash or defensives.
