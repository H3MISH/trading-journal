## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang leadership crisis** — NVDA down ~8% pre-market after CNBC confirmed the board met Wednesday to discuss ousting Huang. WSJ reports the board is preparing to remove him; Bloomberg says they are working with an outside firm on successors and may name an interim CEO. Law firm Wachtell Lipton was hired for an independent investigation into allegations Huang had an affair with someone suspected by U.S. intelligence of being a Chinese spy. Board meeting extended into Wednesday evening with no announced decision. This is the single biggest AI-trade overhang today and could move the entire sector intraday based on headlines.

2. **Hot October CPI reignites Fed hawkishness** — Headline CPI +0.4% (est +0.3%), core CPI +0.3% (est +0.2%), a third consecutive upside miss on core. Fed Governor Waller endorsed a December hike; Dallas Fed Logan said hikes could come "more rapidly." CME FedWatch now prices ~87% probability of a December rate hike. Deutsche Bank upgraded its forecast to three consecutive hikes. The 10-year Treasury briefly broke above 5% (closed ~4.98%); the 2-year jumped ~19 bps to ~4.73%. Rate headwind for growth/AI multiple names is real.

3. **AMD and AVGO bucking the NVDA selloff** — AMD +3.3–3.8% and AVGO +2.6–3.1% pre-market despite NVDA's plunge. William Blair upgraded AMD to Outperform, citing custom AI chip opportunity and share gains from NVDA. The divergence signals an intra-sector rotation: market is differentiating between NVDA-specific GPU risk and the broader custom ASIC / AI networking thesis.
