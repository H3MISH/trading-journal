## Market Recap

Friday's session was a partial recovery from Thursday's historic AI-and-chip selloff — the worst day for semiconductors since 2020, with the SOXX -12.15% and the Nasdaq -5.52% on Nov 5. The October jobs report (165K payrolls added, roughly in line with expectations) gave buyers cover to step back into beaten-down names, lifting the Nasdaq ~1.4% and the S&P 500 ~0.4% while the Dow slipped ~0.9% as cyclicals and defensives lagged. Chip stocks led the bounce but clawed back only an estimated 25–30% of Thursday's losses, leaving the sector deeply oversold going into the weekend.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX est. +3–5%) bounced hard while mega-cap tech (GOOGL +1.2%, MSFT flat) only partially participated, and defensives (WMT -1.6%, COST ~-2%) underperformed sharply. **Read: this was an AI/semi-specific dip-buy funded by trimming defensives** — not a broad risk-on rally. Money rotated back into beaten-down chip names after a capitulation-flavored Thursday, while safe-haven positioning was unwound. MRVL's intraday fade (from +9% open to +4.6% close) is an early warning that the bounce may lack follow-through conviction.
