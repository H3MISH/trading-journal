## Market Recap

US equities closed mixed-to-positive Wednesday as mega-cap software and consumer tech surged while the semiconductor sector absorbed another wave of selling pressure: the S&P 500 gained +0.38% (7,572), the Nasdaq added +0.62% (26,269), and the Dow rose +0.29% (+150 pts). ## Rotation Tracker

**Clear rotation out of AI hardware/memory, back into mega-cap software.** SOXX lagged badly while GOOGL/MSFT/AAPL/META each gained 3–4%, the widest semi-vs.-software divergence since the June post-AVGO earnings selloff. The mechanism: Samsung's Q2 posted a 19× profit surge that beat estimates — yet still disappointed elevated AI expectations, sustaining a sell-the-news cycle that's now pressuring MRVL, MU, and AVGO for a second week. Money that left chip names is rotating into software and consumer tech where AI monetization is visible in the income statement (advertising, cloud, services) rather than in promised future capex.
