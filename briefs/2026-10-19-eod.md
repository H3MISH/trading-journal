# EOD Market Brief — Monday, October 19, 2026

## Market Recap

U.S. equities staged a tech-led rebound on Monday after the worst week for semiconductors since March 2025, with the Nasdaq Composite rising 1.11% to 26,430.89 while the S&P 500 gained 0.41% to 7,690.73 and the Dow slipped 0.07% to 51,966.03. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) snapped an eight-session losing streak — its longest since October 2008 — with the sector recovering roughly $250B in market cap as lower Treasury yields and easing crude oil prices drew in dip buyers. Tech led; defensives and energy lagged.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) +2.4–3.1% >> Nasdaq 100 +1.9% >> Mega-cap tech (GOOGL/MSFT/AAPL) ~flat >> Defensives (COST/WMT) lagged >> Energy/Power (VST) −2.1%.**

Money rotated decisively back into AI semiconductor names after the 8-day, $2T rout — classic dip-buy behavior in a crowded, levered trade. Mega-cap tech barely participated; GOOGL, MSFT, and AAPL all underperformed the Nasdaq 100, suggesting the rebound was concentrated in the more-beaten-down chip names rather than a broad "risk-on" rotation. Defensives didn't attract new money. The key question is whether this is a one-day relief rally or genuine resumption of the AI trade — META and NFLX earnings this week will determine that.
