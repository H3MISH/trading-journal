# Pre-Market Brief: October 2, 2026

## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Hormuz / Oil Risk Premium** – Brent crude ~$91/bbl as Iran's latest demands cloud any Strait of Hormuz reopening. Roughly 5.7M bpd of shut-in production expected through Q4. Combines with a 10-year Treasury yield at 5.24% (eased from mid-week spike but still near multi-decade highs; 5-year briefly topped 5% for first time since 2007) to create a persistent macro headwind for high-multiple names.

2. **MRVL vs. AVGO: Google's Custom AI Chip Pivot** – Marvell surged ~11% after winning a major Google custom ASIC deal; Broadcom fell 6–10% on direct competition fears. This is an *intra-sector* reshuffling of AI custom-chip wallet share, not a broad retreat from the AI trade. Both names volatile on the open.

3. **AMD's World Labs Acquisition** – AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for ~$8.2B all-stock, adding spatial intelligence and robotics capability to narrow the software/system gap with Nvidia. HPE separately secured a $1.2B Vultr order for AMD Helios AI rack systems. AMD up 1.6% pre-market; 35% monthly run raises the bar for the stock to hold gains.
