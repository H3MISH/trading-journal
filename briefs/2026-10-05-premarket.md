## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) vs. Mega-Cap Tech vs. Defensives:**

SOXX enters today up **89% YTD** and +11.3% in September alone — the best-performing industry in the U.S. market in 2026. The AI chip rally has been indiscriminate and broad. 

Mega-cap tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) has lagged semis this year. Morgan Stanley's thesis is that the market mispriced who captures AI economics — arguing platform companies are undervalued vs. chip suppliers at current levels. This is a mean-reversion rotation argument, not a bearish one.

Defensives (COST, WMT) have underperformed materially in 2026's risk-on environment. No near-term catalyst to change that.

**Read:** Money is broadly staying IN the AI trade, not rotating out. The intra-AI rotation has been from pure Nvidia/hardware names toward custom silicon (MRVL, AVGO) and AI platform monetization plays (META). Defensives are dead weight in this environment.
