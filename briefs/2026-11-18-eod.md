# EOD Brief: November 18, 2026

## Market Recap

Nvidia's blowout fiscal Q3 report (revenue $115.8B, +103% YoY; data center +110% to $107.6B) failed
to lift markets as investors concluded the AI trade is priced in and sold into the news. The Nasdaq
shed 2.5% to 24,509, the S&P 500 fell 1.9% to 7,416, and the Dow slumped 1.4% to 51,608 —
a broad risk-off day, not just a chip story. Compounding the pressure: Nvidia disclosed it is
halting shipments of US-approved AI chips to China, expanding the geopolitical overhang across the
entire semiconductor complex.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX ~$141, ~-0.5%)** are in the worst two-month drawdown since 2008 — down 25.5% since
end of September — and today extended the damage despite NVDA's earnings beat, as the market
re-rated the AI chip complex on valuation. AVGO was the lone semi name that caught a bid (+2.4%),
confirming the custom-silicon/networking sub-theme holds even as pure GPU plays suffer.
Mega-cap tech (AAPL ~-1.1%, Nasdaq -2.5%) fell in sympathy; defensives (COST, WMT) showed
relative stability with no confirming 2%+ drops. This looks like generalized risk-off rather
than a clean rotation — money left AI names but no single sector absorbed the outflows cleanly.
