## Rotation Tracker

**Semis in, mega-cap software out.** SOXX up ~1–3% pre-market while AAPL (-1.63%) and MSFT (-1.20%) closed in the red Wednesday. Institutional flows are chasing AI infrastructure (chips, power) rather than the platforms. The moomoo heatmap described it as "chip equipment leads, megacap tech lags." The catalyst is straightforward: Nvidia's beat validates AI capex, which benefits semiconductor suppliers (AMD, MU, MRVL, AVGO) more directly than the platform companies at this stage of the cycle. META is the exception — still running with chips due to its own AI capex story.

**Risk:** If Fed hawkishness pushes the 10-year above 5.5%, high-multiple chip names (especially MRVL and SMCI) could reverse the morning gap.
