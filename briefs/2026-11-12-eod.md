# EOD Market Brief — November 12, 2026

## Market Recap

The session was dominated by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation after a New York Times report detailed two extramarital affairs, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence officials believe is a Chinese spy. Nvidia (NVDA) plunged as much as 14% intraday before paring losses to roughly 5%, dragging the Nasdaq lower at the open before rivals AMD and Broadcom led a midday recovery that turned the Nasdaq 100 green. The Nvidia crisis simultaneously engulfed Super Micro (SMCI), which fell ~11% on a separate DOJ probe into whether entities linked to Alice Huang (Jensen's wife) diverted SMCI server products to China.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX) went flat after yesterday's +6%, while AMD and AVGO decoupled sharply higher as direct Nvidia substitutes — this is **intra-sector rotation**, not a broad semi exit. Mega-cap tech split: AAPL and GOOGL caught safe-haven and AI-alternative bids (+2–6%), while MSFT and AMZN bled modestly (−2.3%, −1.7%), suggesting money is moving **within** tech toward names perceived as less Nvidia-dependent. Defensives (COST, WMT) were largely unchanged — no broad risk-off panic, just AI-hardware-specific repricing.
