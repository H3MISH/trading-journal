# EOD Market Brief — 2026-08-31

## Market Recap

U.S. equities sold off to start the week as escalating U.S.-Iran military tensions drove oil above $85/bbl and sparked broad risk-off flows across stocks and bonds. The S&P 500 fell 0.33% to 7,686; Nasdaq slipped 0.12% to 26,371; Dow shed 0.70% (−374 pts) to 53,186; the Russell 2000 also declined. In a counterintuitive twist, gold fell ~1% (December futures $4,483) as markets repriced Fed rate-hike risk on energy-driven inflation fears, while oil surged 3%+ after the U.S. struck Iran's Larak Island and Iran launched ballistic/anti-ship missiles at U.S. bases in Jordan — the first exchange since July.
