## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **AI lab CEOs (Amodei, Altman, Musk) publicly called for a slowdown in AI development — chip stocks are in a bear market.** Dario Amodei (Anthropic) published a lengthy essay urging deceleration; Sam Altman agreed; Musk endorsed it on X. Markets are interpreting this as a forward capex-pullback signal for AI infrastructure. AMD is -5.6% pre-market, Nasdaq futures -1.8%, S&P futures -0.8%. Chip stocks are now down ~20% from their June record highs, qualifying as a bear market. Bloomberg notes the long-term infrastructure spend thesis remains intact but that is cold comfort for this session.

2. **FOMC rate decision Wednesday, September 16 at 2:00 PM ET — the next binary event that shapes the week.** The Fed has held at 3.50–3.75% since December 2025, but three FOMC members dissented for a hike at the July meeting. Chair Warsh delivered hawkish remarks at Jackson Hole. Markets now price roughly even odds of hold vs. 25bps hike. With 10-yr yields at 4.98% and oil surging, the risk skews to a hike-shock surprise.

3. **Oil at $102.52 WTI / ~$108 Brent — Saudi Arabia shut its East-West crude pipeline after attacks.** The pipeline is a Strait-of-Hormuz bypass route, and there is no timeline for resumption. Iran-Gulf talks are ongoing in Oman but unresolved. This is a direct inflationary shock into a Fed decision week and adds a geopolitical premium to every energy-sensitive name. The IEA cut its 2026 demand outlook by 2.5M bbl/day simultaneously — so prices are rising on supply disruption, not demand strength.
