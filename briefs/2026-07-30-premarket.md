# Pre-Market Brief — July 30, 2026

## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Fed holds, 30-yr Treasury hits 2007 highs** — The Fed voted 9-3 to hold rates at 3.5%–3.75%; three dissenters wanted a hike. Chair Warsh signaled continued data-dependency and left a September hike on the table. The 30-yr yield surged to 5.23% (highest since 2007), the 10-yr sits at 4.657%, and rate-hike probabilities for September are now ~60%. Rising long rates are a structural headwind for rate-sensitive growth names.

2. **META –9%, MSFT +9% pre-market: the AI earnings trade bifurcates** — META badly missed EPS ($6.18 vs. $7.22 est.), Q2 free cash flow collapsed 91% as expenses jumped 55% YoY while revenue grew only 28%, and Q3 revenue guide came in at the low end of expectations. Microsoft surged on Azure growth of +43% and 30M paid Copilot seats. The market is drawing a sharp line: AI spend that shows up in earnings (MSFT) gets rewarded; AI spend that doesn't (META) gets hammered.

3. **Semiconductors near bear market confirmation** — SOXX is down ~23% in July, flirting with the 20%-from-high threshold that would confirm a bear market. AVGO's soft AI guidance last week was the initial catalyst; AMD, MU, and MRVL all fell 7–8% on Tuesday. AMD earnings on Aug 4 are the next major binary event for the sector.

## Rotation Tracker

Semis (SOXX) are down ~23% in July and near bear market confirmation, while MSFT is up 9% pre-market on Azure strength. Defensives (COST, WMT) are quiet — no news and not showing up as rotation destinations, suggesting this isn't a classic risk-off flight to safety. Semis are the clearest casualty as the "AI picks-and-shovels" trade gets stress-tested by real earnings data.
