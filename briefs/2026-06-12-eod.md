## Rotation Tracker

**Semis outperformed, hyperscalers underperformed, defensives held neutral.** SOXX edged higher (~+0.5–1%) led by AMD's +5% Citi-upgrade surge even as the broader Nasdaq barely closed green. Mega-cap tech (MSFT, AMZN, AAPL) each lost ~2% as the SpaceX IPO created the week's dominant capital gravity well — classic IPO rotation out of comparable large-cap growth. COST and WMT showed no meaningful movement (no news, no catalyst), confirming defensives are in hold mode rather than rotation mode. Net read: **money rotated INTO single-stock semis (AMD) and OUT OF hyperscaler AI, driven by an IPO event rather than a macro shift — the semi thesis is intact but the SpaceX distortion unwinds next week.**
