## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Hot Jobs Report Re-Prices Rate Path** — August payrolls came in at +162K vs. the +53K consensus estimate. That single print pushed the probability of a Fed rate *hike* at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting to ~58% (up ~9 points on the day). The 10-Year Treasury closed at 4.79%, the 30-Year at 5.245%. CPI prints Thursday Sept 11 — that's the last major signal before the Fed decision. Expect rate-sensitive growth/tech to open under pressure Tuesday.

2. **AVGO Blew Out Q3; MRVL Whiffed on Guidance** — Broadcom reported Sept 2 after close: $29.6B revenue (+86% YoY), EPS $3.32, both beats. MRVL reported record Q2 ($2.74B, +37% YoY) but guided weak and gapped down ~10-15% on the news. These two results define the current semi narrative: the custom ASIC buildout is real, but expectations are running far ahead of anything but the most dominant players.

3. **September 11 CPI Is the Week's Defining Event** — Friday's jobs print broke the "Fed on hold" consensus. Sticky inflation on Thursday + hot jobs = meaningful probability of a hike nobody had priced three days ago. If CPI surprises hot, expect a coordinated sell-off in AI semis, SMCI, and long-duration growth names. If CPI comes in cool, the relief rally could be sharp — sector has been holding up into this data.
