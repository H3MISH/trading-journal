# EOD Market Brief — July 8, 2026

---

## Market Recap

Stocks ended the session in a split verdict: Dow -1.1%, S&P 500 -0.3%, Nasdaq +0.2%, as President Trump declared the U.S.-Iran ceasefire memorandum "over" at NATO and threatened renewed strikes, sending WTI crude surging 6.2% to $74.79 and Brent +6.1% to $78.66. The spike triggered a sharp rotation out of interest-rate-sensitive and AI-chip names into energy producers (XOM +1.6%, CVX +1.9%) and defensive sectors, while Broadcom (AVGO) bucked the trend entirely after Apple announced a $30B+ custom-chip deal through 2031. Memory and pure-play semiconductor names absorbed the sharpest selling, with MU and AMD each down 4–7% on residual contagion from Samsung's disappointing earnings earlier in the week.
