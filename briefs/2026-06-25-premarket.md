## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Micron (MU) just dropped the most important earnings print of the AI cycle so far.** Q3 revenue of $41.46B crushed the $35.25B estimate; EPS of $25.11 obliterated the $20.28 consensus. Q4 guidance of $50B ± $1B vaporized expectations of $42.9B, with EPS guide of $30–32 vs $24.80 consensus. HBM production is sold out through end of 2026 with no line of sight on supply catching demand. Stock +12% in AH. This directly reverses the AVGO-induced AI-memory pessimism from June 3 and should lift the entire semi complex at the open.

2. **PCE inflation data due this morning — the Warsh-era circuit-breaker.** April headline PCE was 3.77% YoY; core was 3.29%. At the June 16–17 FOMC meeting, the new dot plot showed 9 of 18 officials penciling in at least one rate hike in 2026, a hawkish reversal from March. September rate hike probability has spiked from 29% → 68% over the past week. A hot PCE print is a headwind that can override the MU tailwind, especially for high-beta semis.

3. **US-Iran interim peace deal (MOU) is pushing oil to pre-war lows, 10Y yields down ~10bp.** The Strait of Hormuz is expected to reopen under the 14-point MOU framework. Crude sliding to pre-conflict levels removes a key inflation tail risk and is easing bond yields. This is a mild but real macro tailwind — deflationary and risk-positive at the margin — though the MOU does not resolve core issues (nuclear, sanctions) and is fragile.

**Primary driver: AI infrastructure cycle reconfirmed by MU.** The biggest risk that had been building since AVGO's June 3 report — that AI infrastructure demand was plateauing — has been emphatically refuted this morning. The PCE print this morning is the fulcrum: cool ≤3.1% core → semi rally extends into the afternoon; hot ≥3.4% core → the macro fear clips the wings on even a MU-strength day.

**Geopolitics: Net positive.** US-Iran MOU plus oil prices back to pre-war levels is a tailwind for yields and inflation expectations.

**Verdict: Risk-On for the semi/AI complex, with a stop at the PCE print.** Hold semi longs, watch PCE at 8:30 AM ET as the intraday circuit-breaker.
