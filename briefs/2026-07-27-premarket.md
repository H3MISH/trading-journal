1. **The mega-cap earnings gauntlet begins: META (Wed), MSFT (Wed), AAPL (Thu), AMZN (Thu).** After Alphabet's post-earnings −7.1% on a capex shock last Tuesday (2026 capex raised to $195–205B, even higher in 2027), the market enters the busiest earnings week of the year with a clear "punish AI spend" disposition. 2. **SOXX has entered bear-market territory (−20%+ from its June record high despite a +84.7% YTD gain).** The pullback is severe and fast. JPMorgan sees a summer buying opportunity ahead; Morgan Stanley warns of continued sector underperformance in Q3. SMCI's massive July 22 print (+26.3% on record $60B+ orders and a margin beat) and VST's $10B AI power JV are bright spots within the wreckage, but the ETF level tells a cautious story.

3. **US-Iran tension pause over the weekend eases the oil/macro pressure that had been driving yields higher.** The 10-year Treasury yield has pulled back ~4bp to 4.64% from near multi-month highs. This is a mild risk-asset tailwind, but it competes with FOMC uncertainty (meeting this week; rates expected on hold) and upcoming macro data (Q2 advance GDP, PCE inflation).

## Sector Temperature: MIXED — Risk-Off for AI Capex Names, Risk-On for AI Infrastructure Execution

**AI/mega-cap capex names: Risk-Off.** The market's post-GOOGL read is clear — elevated AI spend without near-term ROI visibility is a negative re-rating event. META, MSFT, AMZN all face the same test Wednesday–Thursday. Get the guidance wrong and they join GOOGL in the penalty box.

**AI infrastructure (SMCI, VST): Risk-On.** Record orders, margin beats, and concrete power/data center JVs are exactly what the market wants to see. These names are not spending on AI; they are profiting from it right now.

**Semis (SOXX): Cautiously Risk-Off with a potential bottoming signal.** The ETF is in bear-market territory from its June peak. AMD and MU are holding up on fundamental conviction but the ETF-level pressure is real. The AMD August 4 earnings print is the next major signal for the sector.

**Macro: Neutral-to-Mild-Positive for today.** The Iran-US weekend de-escalation pulled the 10-year yield down 4bp to 4.64%. FOMC holds this week with near certainty. The risk to this mild tailwind is hotter-than-expected macro data (PCE, GDP) later in the week.

**Overall verdict:** Position for binary earnings outcomes in META (Wednesday) and CCJ (Thursday) — both have asymmetric gap risk. Keep semi longs in names with fundamental anchors (MU, AVGO, SMCI follow-through) but recognize SOXX-level pressure. Fade UBER until there is clarity on Waymo. VST is the cleanest risk-on trade on the board today.
