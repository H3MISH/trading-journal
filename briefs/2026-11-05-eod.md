# EOD Brief — Thursday, November 5, 2026

---

## Market Recap

Wall Street suffered one of its worst days of the year as Democratic midterm gains
stoked fears of tighter AI-infrastructure regulation and potential data-center
moratoriums in key states. The Nasdaq Composite plunged ~5%—its worst single-day
drop since April 2025—falling into correction territory and erasing all post-election
rally gains; the S&P 500 shed roughly 3–3.5% and the Dow lost more than 1,500 points
intraday. Semiconductor names bore the brunt: the SOX index cratered ~9–11% while oil
rising through $96–$100 briefly cushioned energy plays, underscoring a sharp
risk-off rotation out of AI-exposed hardware into defensives and energy.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) −9 to −11% vs. Mega-cap tech −3 to −5% vs. Defensives ~flat/positive.**

Money rotated *hard out of* AI hardware names (chips, data-center servers) and to a
lesser degree out of mega-cap tech. Defensives (COST, WMT) and energy (XOM, VST)
appear to have served as the destination. This is the second wave of the "Great
Deleveraging" in AI stocks—the first was valuation-driven (cost of capital); today's
driver is political (regulation/buildout risk). Until the regulatory outlook clears,
SOXX relative underperformance vs. mega-cap software is likely to persist.

> **Note:** SOXX also executed a **3-for-1 share split effective November 5, 2026**.
> Any SOXX price comparison to prior days must be split-adjusted.

   futures remain red, that confirms the rotation is durable rather than a one-day
