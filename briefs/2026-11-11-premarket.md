# Pre-Market Brief — Wednesday, November 11, 2026

> **Note:** US bond markets are **closed** today (Veterans Day). Equities trade normally. No fresh Treasury auction or yield data until Thursday.

---

## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **Hot October CPI triggered the largest Treasury selloff since April 2025.** Headline CPI +0.4% MoM (est. +0.3%), core +0.3% MoM (est. +0.2%), adj. YoY ~4.0%. 2-yr yield surged 17–19 bps to 4.74–4.75%; 10-yr briefly punched through 5.00% before settling at 4.99%. Fed Governor Waller said 25 bps December hike is live and 50 bps "cannot be ruled out." Rate futures now fully price December. This is the dominant macro headwind going into today.

2. **AMD ripped on a blockbuster Google deal + $2.5B Anthropic bet.** Google Cloud selected AMD's 5th-gen EPYC processors as host chips for its TPU 8tx AI system (TPU 8tx is a next-gen Google accelerator; AMD provides the CPU horsepower). Stock surged 9–13% on Monday, closed ~$511 on Tuesday. AMD also disclosed a $2.5B investment in Anthropic (non-voting shares, via amended Anthropic IPO filing). Two consecutive catalysts in three days. This is the clearest near-term share-gain narrative from the Nvidia leadership vacuum.

3. **NVDA CEO scandal deepens; sector-wide overhang remains.** On Nov 8, Nvidia's board launched an independent investigation after Jensen Huang admitted an extramarital affair with an executive whom US intelligence determined was a Chinese intelligence asset. NVDA fell 4.3% Monday even as the broader semi sector surged. Congressional committees requested a closed-door briefing. November 17 NVDA earnings — now ~4 trading days away — are a live gap event for the entire semi tape.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semi (SOXX) vs. Mega-Cap Tech (GOOGL, MSFT, AAPL) vs. Defensives (COST, WMT)**

- **Semis:** SOXX +7.49% Monday — the SOX index has now recovered ~11% in two sessions off its cycle lows (−38% from June peak). The bounce is real but fragile: NVDA lagged Monday (−4.3% on the scandal), and Tuesday's CPI data modestly cooled the bid.
- **Mega-cap tech:** GOOGL −0.27%, MSFT −2.2%, AAPL −0.6% on Tuesday. The rate-shock read-through. Mega-cap names face the double pressure of higher discount rates AND reduced enterprise IT spend if the economy cools.
- **Defensives:** No specific data for COST/WMT today, but the yield spike typically lifts defensives relative to growth — expect mild outperformance if Tuesday's pattern carries over.

**Read:** This looks like a **selective semi rotation** (AI-chip-specific stories like AMD/Google), not a broad macro risk-on. Money is coming back into semis story by story, not via a broad sector rotation. Mega-cap tech is under rate pressure. The NVDA overhang is the wildcard that keeps the semi bounce from being conviction-driven.
