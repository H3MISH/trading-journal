# EOD Market Brief — 2026-08-07

## Market Recap
The S&P 500 finished nearly flat (SPY -0.06%, QQQ -0.07%) as a shock July jobs report —
payrolls fell 23,000 vs. +80,000 expected, with an additional 103,000 in prior-month
downward revisions — reshuffled Fed rate expectations mid-session. The initial read was
dovish (rate-hike odds collapsed, gold surged 3%, silver +6%), but equity gains faded as
growth-slowdown fears offset the relief. Semiconductors outperformed the tape, with SOXX
closing up ~+1.7% to ~$538, lifted by Marvell's AI memory product unveil and a massive
SMCI pre-earnings squeeze.
