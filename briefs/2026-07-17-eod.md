# EOD Brief — Friday, July 17, 2026

---

## Market Recap

US equities sank for the second consecutive session as a multi-continent AI hardware selloff deepened: China's Moonshot AI unveiled Kimi K3 — billed as the world's largest publicly available model — at the World AI Conference in Shanghai, stoking fears that hyperscalers will trim AI infrastructure spending. An Alphabet report of delays in Gemini 3.5 Pro delivery added fuel to the narrative. The S&P 500 fell 1.01% to 7,457.69, the Nasdaq dropped 1.4% to 25,520, and the Dow shed 0.77% to 52,146; on the week, S&P was -1.6%, Nasdaq -2.9%. A simultaneous oil spike on fresh US-Iran tensions kept energy names elevated while the rest of the market bled.
