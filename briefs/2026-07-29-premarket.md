## Rotation Tracker

**Semis (SOXX) vs. Mega-Cap Tech vs. Defensives:**

Semis are in full retreat — SOXX -23% in July is not a rotation, it is capitulation on the AI chip narrative. The selling is indiscriminate across AMD, MU, MRVL, NVDA, and even TSM (-3.5%). Mega-cap tech is bifurcating: hyperscalers that *spend* AI capex (GOOGL, META, MSFT) are under pressure from ROI skepticism but recovering on dips, while SMCI and server infrastructure names with tangible backlog data (like SMCI's $60B) are outperforming. Defensives (COST, WMT) not yet seeing meaningful inflows per available data — this is a sector-specific de-risking, not a full flight to safety.

**Read:** Rotation out of AI chip infrastructure names, holding pattern in mega-cap tech ahead of MSFT/META prints tonight. If both beat cleanly and raise capex, semis stabilize; if either misses or guides conservatively, SOXX retests lows.
