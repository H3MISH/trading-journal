## Market Recap

The Fed delivered its first rate hike since 2023 — a 25 bps increase to 3.75%–4.00% — and markets exhaled: S&P 500 rose ~0.59% to 7,596 and the Nasdaq climbed as investors bought the decision after Wednesday's initial shock sell-off. Treasury 10-year yields snapped an 8-day rising streak and the VIX collapsed more than 11%, restoring risk appetite. Chipmakers led the charge, with the semiconductor gauge surging ~3% as AI-infrastructure names attracted heavy rotation inflows.

## Rotation Tracker

**Semis crushed everything else today.** SOXX up 2–3%, individual chip names up 3–5% (MU +5.4%, MRVL +5.0%, AMD +4%, SMCI +3.4%) while mega-cap tech (GOOGL +0.66%, MSFT +1.01%, AAPL +0.99%) barely moved and defensives (COST -0.38%, WMT flat) were left behind. Classic post-rate-hike "buy the risk" rotation: money flowed hard into AI-semiconductor and infrastructure plays, not into the software/consumer staples names. The Fed hike is now priced; the next move for semis depends on MU's Sep 22 print.

## What to Watch Tomorrow (Sep 18)

1. **MU pre-earnings positioning** — up 5.4% today into Sep 22 print; watch whether momentum holds or fades into the report. Buy-the-rumor, sell-the-news risk is elevated given 8 straight EPS beats already baked in.
2. **AVGO recovery pace** — stock is 30% off ATH and bouncing; monitor whether Q4 guidance fears (revenue miss vs. consensus) continue to weigh or the AI demand story reasserts.
3. **10-year yield follow-through** — snapped 8-day streak today; a continued yield decline would extend the semi/growth rally; a reversal back toward 2007-era highs reasserts pressure.
4. **Fed speakers** — with 16 of 18 FOMC members penciling in at least one more 2026 hike, any hawkish commentary tomorrow can shift the tone fast.
5. **Post-Fed sector rotation staying power** — if defensives (COST, WMT) remain flat while semis hold gains, rotation is real. If semis give back >1% on Friday, today was a dead-cat bounce.
6. **Dollar strength** — Fed hike "lifted the dollar" (Saxo); a stronger USD is a mild headwind for semi names with high international revenue (AMD, AVGO, MU).
