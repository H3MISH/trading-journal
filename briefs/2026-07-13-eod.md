# EOD Brief: July 13, 2026

## Market Recap

U.S. equities fell broadly Monday as two converging shocks crushed risk appetite: SK Hynix
cratered ~15% in Seoul after KIS analysts cut Q2 profit estimates 8% below consensus on slow
HBM4 shipments, triggering circuit breakers on the KOSPI (-8-9%) and igniting a global
semiconductor selloff; simultaneously, Trump re-engaged the Iran blockade after the weekend
ceasefire and MOU collapsed, sending Brent crude up ~4% to $78.82 and WTI to $74.01 per
barrel. The Nasdaq led declines at -1.55% (25,873) while the Dow lost only -0.26% (52,499) as
energy stocks cushioned it; the S&P 500 settled -0.79% at 7,516.
