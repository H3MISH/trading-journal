## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **AI capex fears + META earnings hangover.** META fell 7% on Oct 22 after posting record revenue but negative free cash flow — capex exceeded operating cash flow for the first time since 2012. The market is now in "show me the money" mode on AI spend. This overhang will pressure the entire AI trade.

2. **Oil spike on Middle East escalation.** Brent topped $108/barrel and WTI is above $97, driven by missile strikes on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. Simultaneously a risk-off trigger for growth/tech and an inflation/rate-cut-delay headwind.

3. **Semis attempting to stabilize after brutal Oct 22 selloff.** SOXX closed ~$475.69 after chip names fell 3–9%; pre-market indications put SOXX at ~480–484, suggesting a shallow dip-buy attempt. The question is whether this is a real floor or a dead-cat bounce.

## Sector Temperature: RISK-OFF (Tentative Bounce in Semis)

**Reasoning:**
- Oil above $97 WTI / $108 Brent = inflation risk, rate-cut-delay concern, consumer headwind
- META's negative FCF crystallized AI capex skepticism — the entire sector is repricing "spend vs. return"

- **Bottom line:** Sellers remain in control; bounces should be treated as selling opportunities until the AI capex narrative stabilizes. Key inflection points: GOOGL/MSFT/AMZN earnings next week and AMD on Nov 3.
