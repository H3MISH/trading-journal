# EOD Market Brief — September 28, 2026

## Market Recap

President Trump rejected Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, sending Brent crude above $107/bbl (+2.5%) and driving the 10-year Treasury yield to 5.24%—its highest since 2007—while the 30-year reached 5.56%, its highest since 2004. The oil-and-yield shock crushed high-multiple AI/growth names: the S&P 500 fell ~0.8% to ~7,743, the Nasdaq slid ~0.9%, and SOXX dropped roughly 3% as AMD fell 3.7% and META shed 4.4%. Energy was the lone standout, with Brent's surge lifting XOM and related names while the rest of the market repriced for a stagflationary geopolitical impulse.
