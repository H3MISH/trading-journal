## Market Recap

Anthropic priced its historic IPO at $83 and opened at $111.50 (ticker: ANT), raising $81.3B at a ~$1.5T valuation — the largest tech IPO on record — but the AI euphoria collapsed into broad selling by midday. The Dow shed ~1,000 points (~−2%), the S&P 500 fell ~1.91% to ~7,184, and the Nasdaq dropped ~2.2% to ~23,912, extending its losing streak to a fifth session. The session was defined by the collision of peak AI optimism and peak AI regulatory risk: short-seller Hunterbrook Capital published a China-export-violation report on AMD, re-igniting fears that Nvidia's export woes (Jensen Huang resigned Nov 12) are a sector-wide problem, not an isolated one.
