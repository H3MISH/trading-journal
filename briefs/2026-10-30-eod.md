# EOD Market Brief — Friday, October 30, 2026

## Market Recap
Intel's catastrophic Q3 earnings miss and PC-cycle guidance cut triggered a broad semiconductor selloff Friday, wiping roughly $1 trillion from chip-sector market caps as the Nasdaq plunged ~2.8% — its worst single session since June. Amazon reported a Q3 top-and-bottom-line miss after the close, threatening to extend the tech rout into Monday. The S&P 500 finished down ~1.3%, with semis the clear epicenter of pain while non-AI tech (META, UBER) held up comparatively better.

## Rotation Tracker
Semis (SOXX -9.4%) were utterly crushed versus every other segment today — defensives (COST, WMT) likely off only ~0.5–1%, representing a 8–9 percentage-point spread. Money did **not** rotate back into AI semis — it rotated out and some parked in cash or defensives. The MSFT Azure beat is the one counterpoint, but it wasn't enough to lift sentiment.
