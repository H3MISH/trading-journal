## Market Recap

US equities pulled back Tuesday as elevated oil prices and rising Treasury yields weighed on sentiment, with tech and semiconductors leading the decline: the S&P 500 fell 0.6%, the Dow dipped 0.2%, and the Nasdaq dropped 1.3%. A closely watched semiconductor gauge (SOXX) sold off roughly 5.5%, the sharpest sector move of the session, on a mix of AI-financing concerns, China competition worry, and pre-earnings caution ahead of AVGO's Q3 report in just 10 days. The VIX climbed ~3.8% and 10-year yields rose 1.79%, signaling investors are shifting to a risk-off posture ahead of Wednesday's FOMC minutes release.
