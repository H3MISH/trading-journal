U.S. stocks closed mixed on Wednesday to finish a volatile September as rising Treasury yields weighed on sentiment — the S&P 500 fell 0.3% to 7,651.54 while the Nasdaq edged up 0.2% to 26,861.06 on narrow tech leadership. MU will likely gap up meaningfully at tomorrow's open; watch for sympathy lifts in AMD, MRVL, and SMCI.

**GOOGL +2% AH** — Alphabet unveiled Gemini 4 Argon, its next-generation flagship AI model, signaling accelerated deployment across Google Cloud and enterprise AI workflows. This is a meaningful AI narrative catalyst heading into tomorrow.

**Nasdaq 100 futures +1.39%** — The combination of MU's blowout and GOOGL's AI reveal puts the overnight setup firmly in the bulls' favor for tech and semis.

3. **GOOGL Gemini 4 reaction:** AH strength into the open; if the model launch generates broader AI enthusiasm, META and other AI-levered names benefit.

4. **AAPL follow-through:** Today's -2.66% decline was notable. Watch whether institutional buyers step in at support or selling extends — if AAPL continues lower it will weigh on the broad indices.

5. **VST:** Stock remains under sustained pressure. Any power-demand news, grid capacity update, or nuclear contracting announcement could be a sharp catalyst given the elevated short interest and discounted valuation.

6. **Treasury yields:** The ongoing upward yield pressure was today's headwind. If NFP disappoints and yields pull back, rate-sensitive names (VST, CCJ, dividend payers) get relief. A yield spike on a hot print would renew pressure on the high-multiple names.
