# Pre-Market Brief — July 16, 2026

## Top 3 Things That Matter Today

1. **TSMC Q2 Earnings (Reporting Today):** Record $40.2B revenue; capex raised to $60–64B (from $52–56B), well above expectations. AI chips now 61% of TSMC revenue. Strong demand confirmation, but the capex escalation is igniting a "spending sustainability" debate that is the dominant narrative weighing on semi stocks in pre-market.

2. **Uber acquires Delivery Hero for $14.8B:** Announced this morning. Cash offer at €41.50/share, combining to 99 global markets and $236B in 2025 gross bookings. Major M&A event; watch for deal-financing and regulatory headline risk on UBER today.

3. **HBM export restriction fears hit semis hard:** MU and MRVL both fell ~7% yesterday on reports the U.S. government is considering unilateral restrictions on high-bandwidth memory exports. AMD is another 2.5% lower pre-market today. Broad semi sector is under compounding pressure: geopolitical risk stacked on top of TSMC capex sustainability concerns.

## Tier 1 Watchlist

**AMD — FLAG (>3% down risk):** Pre-market ~-2.5% on TSMC capex narrative. UBS raised PT to $700 (Buy), Rosenblatt to $665. AI Day event July 22–23 in SF is the next catalyst (Zen 6 EPYC + MI455X GPU roadmap). Q2 earnings August 4. Pullback into AI Day could be noise.

**AVGO:** Pre-market ~$393.25, mild softness. Morgan Stanley reaffirmed Buy, "Core AI Winner." Apple's $30B+ multiyear wireless chip deal anchors the revenue floor. Holding better than peers.

**SMCI — FLAG (>3% risk, structural):** Below $27. $7B equity dilution announced (5B underwritten + $2B ATM) to fund AI server component purchases; also facing a Taiwanese semiconductor export investigation and growing China competition. Dilution overhang is persistent, not a one-day event.

**MU — FLAG (>3% continuation risk):** Fell 6.96% yesterday on HBM export restriction fears. Pre-market still pressured. Q3 already reported (June 24, record results); next earnings Sept 22. Medium-term supported by Anthropic partnership and $22B in long-term agreements across 16 customers. Near-term: politically driven volatility.

**MRVL — FLAG (>3% continuation risk):** Fell 6.62% yesterday; ASIC market competition intensifying as hyperscalers develop in-house chips. Shelf registration filed July 9. Capex "digestion phase" narrative gaining traction among bears. Buy consensus (28 analysts) but technicals are broken near-term.

**META:** Up 3.25% yesterday on AI-driven engagement story and Meta Compute cloud plans ($125–145B 2026 capex). Pre-market flat to slight give-back. **Earnings July 29 — earnings gap risk approaching.** Near-term momentum is positive; the print will define the next leg.

**VST:** Steady. PJM capacity secured, positioned as preferred power provider for AI data centers. Q3 earnings Aug 7. No major news today. Quiet hold.

**UBER — FLAG (>3% move risk today):** $14.8B Delivery Hero acquisition announced this morning; deal extends platform to 99 markets. Stock fell 3.56% on July 14 (likely on deal rumors). Today's official announcement could see continued volatility — bulls cite gross bookings scale, bears cite deal size ($14.8B cash) and integration complexity across 99 markets.

**CCJ:** Down ~4.6% on July 13 on Cigar Lake mine disruption (Orano's McClean Lake sulfuric acid plant offline; Cameco expects ~2 week impact, no 2026 guidance change yet). Trading ~$90.95 vs. analyst avg target $132. **Earnings ~July 30 — watch production commentary.** Uranium demand thesis (AI data centers + nuclear power) remains intact.

SOXX (semis) underperformed, and that gap widens today — SOXX pre-market at $563, down from Wednesday's session high of $578, as TSMC's capex surprise adds a new uncertainty layer.
