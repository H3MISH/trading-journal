# Pre-Market Brief — 2026-06-16

## Top 3 things that matter today

1. **FOMC meeting begins today, decision tomorrow (Wed June 17, 2pm ET).** Rates are widely expected to hold (~89% prediction-market odds), but the real risk is a bias shift from "leaning toward easing" to neutral/hawkish given inflation at 4.2%. New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference is the wildcard — could reverse the AI-trade rally fast if he leans hawkish.
2. **Yesterday's risk-on explosion was geopolitical, not fundamental.** The US-Iran peace deal and reopening of the Strait of Hormuz (ending a 3-month effective closure) sent oil down ~5%+ and the 10-year yield to 4.41%, igniting a broad rally: Nasdaq +3.1%, S&P +1.7%, Dow +0.9% on Monday. Today's futures are essentially flat (Dow +0.13%, Nasdaq +0.03%), suggesting consolidation rather than follow-through — watch for the move to stall or reverse if FOMC tone disappoints.
3. **Idiosyncratic AI/semi catalysts compounded the macro tailwind Monday**: AMD's MEXT acquisition (+7%), Micron's massive analyst price-target hikes (+10.8%), and Marvell's S&P 500 inclusion (+5.9–7.5%) all fired on the same day — meaning today's open is digesting an unusually large one-day move across the whole watchlist.

## Rotation tracker

Monday's move was risk-on across the board — semis, mega-cap AI plays, and even uranium (CCJ) all rallied together on the same geopolitical catalyst, with energy (XOM) the lone sector loser as the oil premium unwound. This isn't a classic "out of AI, into defensives" rotation; it's a broad de-risking-of-geopolitical-tail-risk rally. Today's flat futures point to a holding pattern, not reversal — true test is the FOMC tone tomorrow, not a sector rotation signal today.

## Sector temperature: Risk-on, holding pattern into FOMC

Yesterday's rally was real and broad, but today's flat futures show the market pausing to see what the Fed does Wednesday. With inflation at 4.2% and a still-strong labor market, a hawkish bias shift from new Chair Warsh is the single biggest threat to extending Monday's AI/semis rally. Until that's resolved, expect range-bound, lower-conviction trading with elevated single-stock volatility (MU, AMD, MRVL, SMCI all had outsized idiosyncratic catalysts this week).
