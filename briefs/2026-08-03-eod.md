Stocks rallied broadly on August 3 as oil prices cratered more than 5% after President Trump announced renewed Iran nuclear talks, removing a significant geopolitical risk premium. ## Rotation Tracker

**Semis vs. Mega-Cap Tech vs. Defensives**

Today's action is a textbook earnings-driven rotation: META's +6.22% earnings surge pulled GOOGL (+3.49%), AMZN (+5.22%), and MSFT (+2.30%) higher as investors read through strong ad-market and AI monetization signals. Meanwhile, SOXX *declined* on a day Nasdaq was +2%+ — MU (-4.9%) led chip weakness on memory demand fears, and AMD flat-to-lower as traders de-risk into Tuesday's print. This is a "show me the earnings" rotation: money is moving from AI-chip hardware names (where expectations are sky-high and forward earnings multiples stretched) into software/social names that just delivered. No data on defensives (COST, WMT) today but the oil-price collapse should be a minor tailwind for consumer-facing names.

**Read:** Rotation OUT of chip hardware and INTO mega-cap software/social. Not a broad risk-off; the Nasdaq is up. Watch whether AMD's results tomorrow reverse or reinforce the semi weakness.
