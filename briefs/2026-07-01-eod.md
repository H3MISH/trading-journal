# EOD Market Brief — July 1, 2026

## Market Recap

Markets kicked off Q3 with a strong session as the Nasdaq surged +1.52% to 26,213, the S&P 500 gained +0.52% to 7,499, and the Dow edged +0.27% to a new all-time closing high of 52,319 — capping the best quarter since Q2 2020 (Nasdaq +21.4%, S&P +14.9% for Q2). Semiconductors dominated, with the Philly Semiconductor Index up ~3.9% and AI-linked names AMD, MRVL, and META leading the charge. An ADP private payrolls miss (98K vs. 122K expected) kept bond yields subdued and helped tech multiples expand into the session close.

Pattern read: AI infrastructure trade is reasserting after last week's Broadcom-led sell-off; watch if it's a one-day snapback or confirmation of a new leg.
