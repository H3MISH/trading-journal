# EOD Report — August 19, 2026

## Market Recap

US equities snapped a three-day losing streak Wednesday after the Treasury Department
announced increased buybacks of long-dated government debt, driving yields sharply lower
and removing the bond-market overhang that had pressured stocks since last week. The S&P 500
gained 0.31% to ~7,715, the Dow added 0.22% to 53,463, and the Nasdaq rose ~0.2%; July FOMC
minutes released at 2 PM ET carried no hawkish surprises and reinforced the relief rally.
The session's defining event was Marvell Technology's landmark $12.2 billion Google
custom-chip partnership — MRVL surged as much as 12% intraday while Broadcom (AVGO),
previously Google's dominant custom-silicon partner, fell ~5%, creating a sharp divergence
within the semiconductor space that capped SOXX gains even as the broad market recovered.

of SOXX) overwhelmed MRVL's surge (~3% weight), producing a net drag on the index.
**Mega-cap tech (GOOGL +0.77%, MSFT +0.49%)** recovered mildly on the yield relief.
**Defensives (COST, WMT):** No outsized moves; the defensive bid softened as risk appetite
returned but neither name cleared the 2% bar either way.

**Read:** This was a yield-driven relief rally, not a genuine rotation back into AI/semis.
Money returned to rate-sensitive growth (big-tech, UBER, VST) and uranium plays, but the
AVGO/MRVL disruption muddied the semi picture — SOXX underperformed on a day when it
"should" have bounced. Watch whether semis re-establish leadership or continue to lag
as the sector reorders around the new hyperscaler chip landscape.
