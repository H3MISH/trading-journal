MRVL, MU, SMCI all caught contagion. Pre-market June 17 suggests SOXX recovering to ~$604 (+2.2%), but the FOMC at 2 PM is the all-important circuit breaker for whether that hold sticks.

3. **Iran provisional peace deal moderating oil and macro risk — Treasury yields drifting lower.** The 10-year Treasury fell to ~4.44% (down ~13 bps from last week's 4.57% peak), as a provisional US-Iran peace agreement took some energy and inflation risk premium off the table. Lower yields are a relative tailwind for growth/semis, but with May CPI at 4.2% and inflation still above target, the market is not pricing a clean all-clear. The geopolitical risk premium in CCJ and VST may fade if peace holds.
