# US Market EOD — October 8, 2026

## 1. Market Recap

U.S. equities sold off sharply on Thursday, with the **Nasdaq Composite falling -1.25% to 27,193.34** and the **S&P 500 declining -0.47% to 7,765.48**, while the Dow Jones edged up ~+0.10% to ~51,232, reflecting a clean split between tech/AI names and the rest of the market. The dominant catalyst was an **FT report revealing OpenAI's annualized revenue was ~$50B**, roughly $20B below the ~$70B figure that had been widely cited by analysts, triggering broad AI infrastructure spending skepticism across semis, cloud, and AI-adjacent names. Macro headwinds compounded the damage: **10-year Treasury yields hit 5.23%**, the **30-year reached 5.61%** (near a 24-year high), and **Brent crude surged to ~$105/barrel** on escalating Middle East tensions — a stagflation cocktail that punished growth multiples while lifting energy. Market breadth was decisively negative at nearly 3-to-1 decliners over advancers; CNN reported the Nasdaq was on pace for its worst single session since July.

## 6. Rotation Tracker

**SOXX** closed ~-3.5 to -4%, a roughly 3–4x worse outcome than mega-cap software: **GOOGL** ended ~-0.7%, **MSFT** ~flat to slightly positive (~+0.3%), and **AAPL** ~+0.8% — suggesting large-cap consumer tech decoupled meaningfully from pure-play AI infrastructure. Defensives outperformed: **WMT** gained ~+2% intraday and **COST** closed ~+0.5%, consistent with flight-to-stability amid stagflation signals. The rotation was unambiguously **out of AI infrastructure and into energy/consumer defensive**: oil at $105 simultaneously pressured tech multiples (via higher rates) and lifted energy names (XOM +2.84%, sector +2.96%). This is a continuation of a theme building since July 2026 — hyperscaler capex commitments now exceed $195B at Alphabet alone, and with OpenAI's revenue shortfall acting as a data point against AI monetization, the market is beginning to price the risk that AI buildout demand is overstated. The pattern for Oct 8 was **rotate out, hold the AI trade in question**, not a full reversal of the 2026 AI bull thesis.
